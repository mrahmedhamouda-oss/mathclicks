1 ·  The 12-hour and 24-hour clock

Time is the one topic in the course that is not decimal: 60 seconds in a minute, 60 minutes in an hour, 24 hours in a day. Start by being fluent with the two ways of writing a time.

12-hour clock

  • Hours run 12, 1, 2, … 11 and then start again.
  • Always needs am (midnight to noon) or pm (noon to midnight).
  • Written 7.45 pm or 7:45 pm.

24-hour clock

  • Hours run 00 to 23; no am or pm.
  • Always four digits: 08:05, not 8:05.
  • Midnight is 00:00; noon is 12:00.
Converting am times: keep the hours the same, but write midnight hours as 00 (12:30 am = 00:30).
pm times: add 12 to the hours — except 12-something pm, which is already right (12:40 pm = 12:40).
Example 1Write (a) 8:05 pm and (b) 12:15 am in 24-hour clock time; write (c) 03:40 and (d) 19:08 in 12-hour clock time.
1
(a) It is pm and the hour is not 12, so add 12 to the hours. 8 + 12 = 20  →  20:05
2
(b) 12-something am is just after midnight, so the hour becomes 00. 12:15 am → 00:15
3
(c) The hour is less than 12, so it is a morning time. 03:40 → 3:40 am
4
(d) The hour is more than 12, so subtract 12 and write pm. 19 − 12 = 7  →  7:08 pm
(a) 20:05   (b) 00:15   (c) 3:40 am   (d) 7:08 pm
Watch out 12:15 am is a quarter past midnight, while 12:15 pm is a quarter past noon. They are twelve hours apart.

2 ·  Adding and subtracting times

Never do time arithmetic in a straight line on the calculator. Split the journey at the whole hour and count in two easy pieces — that is exactly how the mark scheme expects to see it.

Adding a duration

  1. Add the whole hours first.
  2. Then add the minutes.
  3. If the minutes go past 60, subtract 60 and carry 1 hour.

09:47 + 3 h 40 min → 12:47 → 12:87 → 13:27

Finding a duration

  1. Count from the start time up to the next whole hour.
  2. Count whole hours.
  3. Count the last few minutes.

14:35 → 15:00 is 25 min; 15:00 → 17:00 is 2 h; 17:00 → 17:10 is 10 min → 2 h 35 min

Crossing midnight If the finishing time is earlier on the clock than the starting time, the journey has gone past midnight. Split the count at 24:00 and add the two pieces.
Example 2A nurse begins a night shift at 21:45 and finishes at 06:30 the next morning. How long is the shift?
1
The finish is earlier on the clock than the start, so the shift crosses midnight. Count up to midnight first. 21:45 → 22:00 is 15 min
22:00 → 24:00 is 2 h
so 21:45 → 00:00 is 2 h 15 min
2
Now count from midnight to the finish. 00:00 → 06:30 is 6 h 30 min
3
Add the two pieces, carrying an hour when the minutes reach 60. 2 h 15 min + 6 h 30 min = 8 h 45 min
The shift lasts 8 hours 45 minutes
Exam tip Run the addition back to confirm: 21:45 + 8 h = 05:45, and 05:45 + 45 min = 06:30. ✓

3 ·  Minutes as decimals of an hour

Speed, distance and time questions need the time as a decimal number of hours — and this is where the most common error of the whole measurement topic lives.

The classic mistake 2 h 45 min is not 2.45 hours. There are 60 minutes in an hour, not 100, so 45 min = 4560 = 0.75 h and 2 h 45 min = 2.75 h.

Example 3(a) Write 3 h 24 min as a decimal number of hours. (b) Write 2.15 hours in hours and minutes.
1
(a) Only the minutes change; divide them by 60. 24 ÷ 60 = 0.4
2
Put the whole hours back on the front. 3 h 24 min = 3.4 h
3
(b) The 2 is 2 whole hours. Multiply the decimal part by 60. 0.15 × 60 = 9 min
(a) 3.4 hours    (b) 2 hours 9 minutes
Watch out 2.15 h is not 2 h 15 min. Two hours and fifteen minutes would be 2.25 h.

4 ·  Timetables and speed

A timetable is a table of 24-hour clock times: each column is one bus or train, each row is one place. To find a journey time, read the two times in the same column and find the difference. Then, when speed enters the question, make the units agree before substituting.

speed = distancetime    distance = speed × time    time = distancespeed If speed is in km/h the time must be in hours
Example 4A train leaves at 09:20 and travels 210 km at an average speed of 56 km/h. At what time does it arrive?
1
Find the journey time in hours. time = 210 ÷ 56 = 3.75 h
2
Convert the decimal back into hours and minutes. 0.75 × 60 = 45  →  3 h 45 min
3
Add the duration to the departure time. 09:20 + 3 h = 12:20;  12:20 + 45 min = 13:05
The train arrives at 13:05

1

The film finishes at 22:15

Common mistakes Lose no easy marks

Treating time as decimal 10:50 + 25 min is not 10:75. Once the minutes reach 60 you carry an hour: 10:75 = 11:15. Subtracting works the same way in reverse — borrow 60, not 100.
Reading 2.45 h as 2 h 45 min The digits after the decimal point are hundredths of an hour, not minutes. 2 h 45 min = 2.75 h, and 2.45 h = 2 h 27 min.
Mixing up 12 am and 12 pm 12:15 am is just after midnight (00:15); 12:15 pm is just after noon (12:15). Twelve hours apart.
Exam tip For any duration question, split the count at the next whole hour (and at midnight if you cross it), and show the pieces. The method mark lives in that splitting — and it makes the arithmetic almost impossible to get wrong.
