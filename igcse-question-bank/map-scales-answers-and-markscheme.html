Question 1  [2 marks]
Work out the actual distance between the two villages. Give your answer in kilometres.
  1. A scale of 1 : 50 000 means 1 cm on the map represents 50 000 cm on the ground.
  2. Actual distance = 8 × 50 000 = 400 000 cm — multiply the map distance by the scale factor
  3. 400 000 ÷ 100 = 4000 m — 100 cm = 1 m
  4. 4000 ÷ 1000 = 4 km — 1000 m = 1 km

Question 5  [4 marks]
(a)Show that an area of 1 cm2 on the map represents an actual area of 4 × 104 m2.
  1. A length scale factor of 20 000 gives an area scale factor of 20 0002. — areas scale by the square of the length scale factor
  2. 1 cm2 represents 20 0002 = 4 × 108 cm2.
  3. 1 m2 = 1002 = 10 000 cm2, so divide by 10 000.
  4. 4 × 108 ÷ 104 = 4 × 104 m2 as required.
(b)A forest has an area of 12 cm2 on the map. Calculate the actual area of the forest, in km2.
  1. Actual area = 12 × 4 × 104 = 480 000 m2
  2. 1 km2 = 10002 = 1 000 000 m2.
  3. 480 000 ÷ 1 000 000 = 0.48 km2

Question 7  [4 marks]
(a)The actual office block is 24 m tall. Calculate the height of the model, in centimetres.
  1. 24 m = 24 × 100 = 2400 cm — work in a single unit before using the scale
  2. Model height = 2400 ÷ 150 — the model is 150 times smaller
  3. = 16 cm

Question 9  [5 marks]
(a)The model is 92 cm tall. Calculate the height of the real water tower, in metres.
  1. Real height = 92 × 250 = 23 000 cm — multiply the model length by the scale factor
  2. 23 000 ÷ 100 = 230 m — 100 cm = 1 m
(b)The model holds 32 cm3 of water. Calculate the capacity of the real water tower, in m3.
  1. The length scale factor is 250, so the volume scale factor is 2503. — volumes scale by the cube of the length scale factor
  2. 2503 = 15 625 000
  3. Real capacity = 32 × 15 625 000 = 500 000 000 = 5 × 108 cm3
  4. 1 m3 = 1003 = 1 000 000 cm3.
  5. 5 × 108 ÷ 106 = 500 m3

Question 10  [6 marks]
(a)Calculate the actual length of AC. Give your answer in kilometres, correct to 3 significant figures.
  1. Triangle ABC has a right angle at B, so use Pythagoras' theorem on the map lengths first.
  2. AC2 = 4.82 + 6.52 = 23.04 + 42.25 = 65.29
  3. AC = √65.29 = 8.0802… cm on the map
  4. Actual length = 8.0802… × 25 000 = 202 005… cm
  5. ÷ 100 000 gives 2.02005… km — 1 km = 100 000 cm
  6. AC = 2.02 km (3 s.f.)
(b)Calculate the actual area of the field. Give your answer in km2.
  1. Map area = 1⁄2 × 4.8 × 6.5 = 15.6 cm2 — the two perpendicular sides are the base and the height
  2. Area scale factor = 25 0002 = 6.25 × 108 — areas scale by the square of the length scale factor
  3. Actual area = 15.6 × 6.25 × 108 = 9.75 × 109 cm2
  4. 1 km2 = 100 0002 = 1010 cm2.
  5. 9.75 × 109 ÷ 1010 = 0.975 km2
  6. Check: 15.6 cm2 × 0.0625 km2 per cm2 = 0.975 ✓ — since 1 cm represents 0.25 km, 1 cm2 represents 0.0625 km2
