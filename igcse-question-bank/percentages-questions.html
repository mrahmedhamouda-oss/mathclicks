(a)

$[1]
(b)

$[2]
(c)

%[2]

In the second year the value increases by a further 4%.

(a)
Calculate the value of the house at the end of the second year.
$[2]
(b)
Calculate the overall percentage increase in the value of the house over the two years.
%[2]
(c)
