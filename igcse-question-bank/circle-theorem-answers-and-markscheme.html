Question 1  [2 marks]
(a)Find angle ABC.
  1. Angle AOC = 126° is the angle at the centre standing on arc AC. — given
  2. Angle ABC = 126 ÷ 2 = 63° — the angle at the centre is twice the angle at the circumference standing on the same arc
(b)Find angle OAC.
  1. OA = OC, so triangle OAC is isosceles. — both are radii
  2. Angle OAC = (180 − 126) ÷ 2 = 27° — angle sum of a triangle; base angles of an isosceles triangle are equal

Question 5  [4 marks]
(a)Write down angle OPT.
  1. TP is a tangent and OP is the radius drawn to the point of contact P.
  2. Angle OPT = 90° — a tangent is perpendicular to the radius at the point of contact
(b)Find angle POQ.
  1. OPTQ is a quadrilateral with angle OPT = angle OQT = 90° and angle PTQ = 46°.
  2. Angle POQ = 360 − 90 − 90 − 46 = 134° — the angles of a quadrilateral add up to 360°
(c)Find angle PQT.
  1. TP = TQ, so triangle TPQ is isosceles. — the two tangents from an external point are equal in length
  2. Angle PQT = (180 − 46) ÷ 2 = 67° — angle sum of a triangle; base angles are equal
(d)Find angle PRQ.
  1. Angle POQ = 134° is at the centre and angle PRQ is at the circumference, both on arc PQ.
  2. Angle PRQ = 134 ÷ 2 = 67° — the angle at the centre is twice the angle at the circumference

  2. Angle BAD = 148 ÷ 2 = 74° — the angle at the centre is twice the angle at the circumference
(b)Find angle BCD, giving a reason for your answer.

  2. Angle BCD = 180 − 74 = 106° — opposite angles of a cyclic quadrilateral add up to 180°

(c)Find angle OBD.
  1. OB = OD, so triangle OBD is isosceles. — both are radii
  2. Angle OBD = (180 − 148) ÷ 2 = 16° — angle sum of a triangle; base angles are equal
