Question 1  [2 marks]
(a)Write down how long Priya waits at the park.
  1. While Priya waits, her distance from home does not change, so the graph is horizontal.
  2. The horizontal section runs from 30 minutes to 50 minutes.
  3. She waits for 50 − 30 = 20 minutes
(b)Work out her walking speed for the first 30 minutes.
Give your answer in km/h.
  1. In the first 30 minutes she walks 3 km. — read the first vertex off the graph
  2. 30 minutes = 30⁄60 = 0.5 hours — the answer is asked for in km/h, so the time must be in hours
  3. Speed = distance ÷ time = 3 ÷ 0.5 = 6 km/h

Question 2  [3 marks]
(a)Write down how long he is stationary during the journey.
Give your answer in minutes.
  1. The graph is horizontal from 1 hour to 1.5 hours, so the car is not moving.
  2. 1.5 − 1 = 0.5 hours = 30 minutes — 0.5 × 60 = 30
(b)Calculate his speed during the last stage of the journey.
  1. The last stage runs from (1.5, 70) to (3, 190). — read both ends of the final straight section
  2. Distance travelled = 190 − 70 = 120 km
  3. Time taken = 3 − 1.5 = 1.5 hours
  4. Speed = 120 ÷ 1.5 = 80 km/h — the speed is the gradient of the distance–time graph

Question 4  [4 marks]

  1. The bus travels 60 km between 08:00 and 09:30.
  2. Time taken = 1.5 hours.
  3. Speed = 60 ÷ 1.5 = 40 km/h — gradient of the bus line
(b)Write down the speed of the motorcycle.
  1. The motorcycle travels 60 km between 08:15 and 09:15.
  2. Time taken = 1 hour.
  3. Speed = 60 ÷ 1 = 60 km/h — the line slopes downwards because the distance from X is decreasing
(c)Find the time at which the motorcycle passes the bus, and the distance of this point from town X.
  1. The two vehicles are at the same place when the two lines cross.
  2. The lines cross at 08:45, level with 30 on the distance axis.
  3. Check algebraically: bus 40t = motorcycle 60 − 60(t − 0.25) gives 100t = 75, so t = 0.75 hours after 08:00.
  4. They pass at 08:45, 30 km from town X

  1. The van covers 84 km between 0 and 1.75 hours. — 1.75 hours is 1 hour 45 minutes

  1. The first stage goes from (0, 0) to (1.5, 6).

(b)Work out her walking speed on the return walk.
  1. The return walk goes from (3, 9) back to (4.5, 0).

Question 8  [4 marks]
(a)Calculate the speed of the train during the first stage.
Give your answer in km/h.
  1. The first stage covers 30 km in 20 minutes.
  2. 20 minutes = 20⁄60 = 1⁄3 hour
  3. Speed = 30 ÷ 1⁄3 = 30 × 3 = 90 km/h
(b)Find the time, in minutes after leaving station A, at which the train is 60 km from station A.
  1. The last stage runs from (25, 30) to (60, 100).
  2. Gradient = (100 − 30) ÷ (60 − 25) = 70 ÷ 35 = 2 km per minute. — working in km per minute keeps the arithmetic simple here
  3. From 30 km, a further 60 − 30 = 30 km is needed, taking 30 ÷ 2 = 15 minutes.
  4. Time = 25 + 15 = 40 minutes
  5. Check: at 40 minutes the graph reads 30 + 2 × 15 = 60 km ✓

Question 9  [5 marks]
(a)Calculate the speed of the van during the first stage.
  1. The first stage covers 60 km between 09:00 and 10:12.
  2. Time taken = 72 minutes = 72⁄60 = 1.2 hours.
  3. Speed = 60 ÷ 1.2 = 50 km/h
(b)The van leaves at 10:30 and travels the rest of the way at a constant speed.
Calculate the smallest speed it can travel at and still reach the customer by 11:30.
  1. Distance still to travel = 105 − 60 = 45 km.
  2. Time available from 10:30 to 11:30 = 1 hour. — using all of the available time gives the smallest possible speed
  3. Speed = 45 ÷ 1 = 45 km/h
(c)Calculate the average speed of the van for the whole journey from 09:00 to 11:30.
  1. Total distance = 105 km and total time = 2.5 hours. — 09:00 to 11:30 is 2 hours 30 minutes
  2. Average speed = 105 ÷ 2.5 = 42 km/h

Question 10  [6 marks]
(a)Calculate her cycling speed during the first stage.
  1. The first stage goes from (10:00, 0) to (10:45, 15).
  2. Time taken = 45 minutes = 0.75 hours.
  3. Speed = 15 ÷ 0.75 = 20 km/h
(b)Calculate her average speed for the whole journey from 10:00 to 11:30.
  1. Total distance = 24 km and total time from 10:00 to 11:30 = 1.5 hours. — the average speed uses the whole journey, including the rest
  2. Average speed = 24 ÷ 1.5 = 16 km/h
(c)Yusuf leaves Mia's home at 10:30 and cycles along the same route at a constant speed of 30 km/h.
Find the time at which Yusuf passes Mia.
  1. Let t be the time in hours after 10:00.
  2. Yusuf starts at t = 0.5, so his distance is 30(t − 0.5). — distance = speed × time travelled
  3. At t = 1, Yusuf has gone 30 × 0.5 = 15 km.
  4. At t = 1 Mia is also 15 km from home, since her rest lasts from 10:45 to 11:00. — read the end of the horizontal section
  5. Yusuf passes Mia at 11:00, 15 km from home
  6. Check: at 10:57 Yusuf has gone 13.5 km and Mia is at 15 km, so he is still behind ✓
