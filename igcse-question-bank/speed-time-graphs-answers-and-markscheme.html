Question 1  [2 marks]
(a)Write down how long the tram travels at a constant speed.
  1. The graph is horizontal from 10 s to 30 s. — a horizontal line means constant speed
  2. Time = 30 − 10 = 20 seconds
(b)Work out the acceleration of the tram during the first 10 seconds.
  1. Acceleration = gradient = change in speed ÷ change in time.
  2. Acceleration = 20 ÷ 10 = 2 m/s2

Question 5  [4 marks]

  1. Split the area under the graph into three parts.
  2. Triangle (0 to 15 s): 1⁄2 × 15 × 24 = 180 m
  3. Rectangle (15 to 35 s): 20 × 24 = 480 m
  4. Trapezium (35 to 50 s): 1⁄2 × (24 + 10) × 15 = 255 m — the car slows down but does not stop

Question 6  [4 marks]
(a)Show that the total distance travelled, in metres, is 40V.
  1. Distance = area under the graph, a trapezium.
  2. The parallel sides are 50 s and 40 − 10 = 30 s, and the height is V m/s.
  3. Area = 1⁄2 × (50 + 30) × V
  4. Distance = 40V metres — as required
  5. Check in three pieces: 5V + 30V + 5V = 40V ✓
(b)The total distance travelled is 720 m.
Find the value of V.
  1. 40V = 720 — using the given distance
  2. V = 720 ÷ 40 = 18
  3. Check: the acceleration is then 18 ÷ 10 = 1.8 m/s2, and 1⁄2 × (50 + 30) × 18 = 720 m ✓

Question 8  [4 marks]
(a)Show that the train takes 15 seconds to stop.
  1. Slowing down at 1.6 m/s2 means the speed falls by 1.6 m/s each second.
  2. Time = change in speed ÷ deceleration = 24 ÷ 1.6
  3. Time = 15 seconds — as required
(b)Calculate the distance the train travels while it is slowing down.
  1. The region under the graph is a triangle of base 15 s and height 24 m/s.
  2. Distance = 1⁄2 × 15 × 24 — distance is the area under the graph
  3. Distance = 180 m
  4. Check: the average speed is (24 + 0) ÷ 2 = 12 m/s, and 12 × 15 = 180 m ✓

Question 9  [5 marks]
(a)Calculate the retardation between 28 seconds and 38 seconds.
  1. Between 28 s and 38 s the speed falls from 20 m/s to 8 m/s.
  2. Change in speed = 20 − 8 = 12 m/s in 10 seconds.
  3. Retardation = 12 ÷ 10 = 1.2 m/s2 — the acceleration is −1.2 m/s2; a retardation is quoted as a positive number
(b)Calculate the total distance travelled in the 48 seconds.
  1. Split the area under the graph into four parts.
  2. Triangle (0 to 8 s): 1⁄2 × 8 × 20 = 80 m
  3. Rectangle (8 to 28 s): 20 × 20 = 400 m
  4. Trapezium (28 to 38 s): 1⁄2 × (20 + 8) × 10 = 140 m
  5. Rectangle (38 to 48 s): 10 × 8 = 80 m
  6. Total distance = 80 + 400 + 140 + 80 = 700 m

Question 10  [6 marks]
(a)Work out the distance travelled during the first 20 seconds.
  1. The first section is a triangle of base 20 s and height 30 m/s.
  2. Distance = 1⁄2 × 20 × 30 = 300 m
(b)Find the value of t.
  1. Last section: 1⁄2 × 40 × 30 = 600 m.
  2. Middle section: 30 × t = 30t metres. — a rectangle
  3. Total distance: 300 + 30t + 600 = 2400
  4. 30t = 1500 — subtract 900 from both sides
  5. t = 50
  6. Check: 300 + 30 × 50 + 600 = 2400 ✓
(c)Calculate the average speed of the train for the whole journey.
Give your answer correct to 3 significant figures.
  1. Total time = 20 + 50 + 40 = 110 seconds.
  2. Average speed = 2400 ÷ 110 = 21.818… m/s — exact value 240⁄11
  3. Average speed = 21.8 m/s (3 s.f.)
