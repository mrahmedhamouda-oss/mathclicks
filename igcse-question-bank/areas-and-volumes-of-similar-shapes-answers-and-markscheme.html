Question 10  [6 marks]
(a)Calculate the volume of the water.
Give your answer correct to 3 significant figures.
  1. The water forms a cone that is similar to the whole container. — the surface of the water is parallel to the top
  2. k = 18⁄24 = 0.75
  3. Volume factor = 0.753 = 0.421875
  4. Volume of water = 1500 × 0.421875 = 632.8125
  5. Volume of water = 633 cm3 (3 s.f.)
(b)More water is poured in until the container is exactly half full.
Calculate the new depth of the water.
Give your answer correct to 3 significant figures.
  1. Half full means the volume of water is 1500⁄2 = 750 cm3.
  2. Volume factor = 750⁄1500 = 0.5, so (h⁄24)3 = 0.5
  3. h⁄24 = 3√0.5 = 0.79370… — take the cube root
  4. h = 24 × 0.79370… = 19.0488…
  5. New depth = 19.0 cm (3 s.f.) — the depth is more than half of 24, as expected
