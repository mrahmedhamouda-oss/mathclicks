Question 1  [2 marks]
(a)Work out the cost of hiring a bicycle for 7 hours.
  1. C = 6h + 15 with h = 7 — substitute the number of hours
  2. C = 6 × 7 + 15 = 42 + 15 — multiply before adding
  3. Cost = $57
(b)Nadia pays $111 to hire a bicycle. Work out the number of hours she hires it for.
  1. 6h + 15 = 111 — substitute C = 111 and form an equation
  2. 6h = 111 − 15 = 96 — subtract 15 from both sides
  3. h = 96 ÷ 6 = 16 hours — divide both sides by 6
  4. Check: 6 × 16 + 15 = 111 ✓

Question 7  [4 marks]
(a)Calculate the volume of a sphere of radius 6.5 cm. Give your answer correct to 3 significant figures.
  1. V = 4⁄3 × π × 6.5³ — substitute r = 6.5
  2. 6.5³ = 274.625 — cube the radius first
  3. V = 4⁄3 × π × 274.625 = 1150.34…
  4. V = 1150 cm³ (3 s.f.)
(b)A different sphere has volume 500 cm³.
Calculate its radius, correct to 3 significant figures.
  1. 4⁄3πr³ = 500 — substitute V = 500
  2. r³ = 3 × 500⁄4π = 119.366… — multiply by 3 and divide by 4π
  3. r = ∛119.366… = 4.9237… — take the cube root
  4. r = 4.92 cm (3 s.f.)
  5. Check: 4⁄3π × 4.9237…³ = 500 ✓

Question 9  [5 marks]
(a)Calculate f when u = 12 and v = 18.
  1. 1⁄f = 1⁄12 + 1⁄18 — substitute u = 12 and v = 18
  2. 1⁄f = 3⁄36 + 2⁄36 = 5⁄36 — common denominator 36
  3. f = 36⁄5 = 7.2 — the reciprocal — do not stop at 5⁄36
(b)Make u the subject of the formula.

  3. u = fv⁄(v − f) — take the reciprocal of both sides
  4. Check: f = 7.2, v = 18 gives u = 7.2 × 18⁄10.8 = 12 ✓

Question 10  [6 marks]
(a)Show that  A = 2πr² + 2V⁄r.
  1. Total surface area = 2 circular ends + curved surface — A = 2πr² + 2πrh
  2. V = πr²h, so h = V⁄πr² — make h the subject of the volume formula
  3. 2πrh = 2πr × V⁄πr² = 2V⁄r — π and one factor of r cancel
  4. A = 2πr² + 2V⁄r — as required
(b)Calculate A when r = 5 and V = 900. Give your answer correct to 3 significant figures.
  1. A = 2π × 5² + 2 × 900⁄5 — substitute r = 5 and V = 900
  2. A = 157.079… + 360 = 517.079…
  3. A = 517 cm² (3 s.f.) — exact value 50π + 360
(c)Calculate V when A = 250 and r = 4. Give your answer correct to 3 significant figures.
  1. 250 = 2π × 4² + 2V⁄4 — substitute A = 250 and r = 4
  2. 2V⁄4 = 250 − 100.530… = 149.469… — subtract 32π from both sides
  3. V = 4 × 149.469…⁄2 = 298.93…
  4. V = 299 cm³ (3 s.f.)
  5. Check: 2π × 16 + 2 × 298.93…⁄4 = 250 ✓
