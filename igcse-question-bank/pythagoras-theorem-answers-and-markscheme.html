Question 1  [2 marks]
Calculate AC.
  1. AC is the hypotenuse, so AC2 = AB2 + BC2 — Pythagoras' theorem: the square on the hypotenuse equals the sum of the squares on the other two sides
  2. AC2 = 82 + 152 = 64 + 225 = 289
  3. AC = √289 = 17 cm

Question 4  [4 marks]

  2. AM = 14 ÷ 2 = 7 cm
  3. In right-angled triangle AMC: CM2 = 252 − 72 — Pythagoras' theorem
  4. CM2 = 625 − 49 = 576, so CM = √576 = 24 cm
(b)Work out the area of triangle ABC.

Question 5  [4 marks]
(a)Calculate the length AB.
  1. Horizontal step from A to B: 5 − (−3) = 8
  2. Vertical step from A to B: 8 − 2 = 6
  3. These two steps meet at a right angle, so AB2 = 82 + 62 = 64 + 36 = 100 — Pythagoras' theorem
  4. AB = √100 = 10
(b)Calculate the length BC. Give your answer correct to 3 significant figures.

  3. BC2 = 42 + 72 = 16 + 49 = 65 — Pythagoras' theorem
  4. BC = √65 = 8.0622… = 8.06 (3 s.f.)

Question 8  [4 marks]
(a)Show that DX = 12 cm.
  1. The trapezium is symmetrical because AD = BC, so the two overhangs are equal.
  2. AX = (18 − 8) ÷ 2 = 5 cm
  3. Triangle AXD has a right angle at X and hypotenuse AD = 13 cm.
  4. DX2 = 132 − 52 = 169 − 25 = 144 — Pythagoras' theorem
  5. DX = √144 = 12 cm
(b)Work out the area of the trapezium.
  1. Area of a trapezium = 1⁄2 × (sum of the parallel sides) × height
  2. Area = 1⁄2 × (18 + 8) × 12 = 13 × 12 = 156 cm2

Question 9  [5 marks]
(a)Show that x2 − 2x − 15 = 0.
  1. AC is the hypotenuse, so BC2 + AB2 = AC2 — Pythagoras' theorem
  2. x2 + (x + 7)2 = (x + 8)2
  3. x2 + x2 + 14x + 49 = x2 + 16x + 64 — expand both squared brackets
  4. 2x2 + 14x + 49 = x2 + 16x + 64 — collect the left-hand side
  5. x2 − 2x − 15 = 0 — subtract x2 + 16x + 64 from both sides
(b)Solve the equation and work out the perimeter of the triangle.
  1. x2 − 2x − 15 = (x − 5)(x + 3) = 0 — −5 × 3 = −15 and −5 + 3 = −2
  2. x = 5 or x = −3; a length cannot be negative, so x = 5.
  3. The sides are 5 cm, 12 cm and 13 cm, so the perimeter = 5 + 12 + 13 = 30 cm
  4. Check: 52 + 122 = 25 + 144 = 169 = 132 ✓

Question 10  [6 marks]
(a)Calculate the length of the diagonal AC. Give your answer correct to 3 significant figures.
  1. ABCD is a square, so angle ABC = 90°.
  2. AC2 = 102 + 102 = 100 + 100 = 200 — Pythagoras' theorem
  3. AC = √200 = 10√2 = 14.142… = 14.1 cm (3 s.f.)
(b)Calculate the vertical height of the pyramid. Give your answer correct to 3 significant figures.
  1. The vertex V is directly above the centre of the square, which is the midpoint of AC. — the pyramid is symmetrical, since all four sloping edges are equal
  2. Half of the diagonal = √200 ÷ 2 = 7.0710… and (√200 ÷ 2)2 = 50
  3. height2 = 132 − 50 = 169 − 50 = 119 — Pythagoras' theorem in the right-angled triangle formed by the height, half the diagonal and a sloping edge
  4. height = √119 = 10.9087… = 10.9 cm (3 s.f.)
(c)Calculate VM.
  1. M is the midpoint of AB, so MB = 5 cm.
  2. Triangle VMB has a right angle at M, because triangle VAB is isosceles with VA = VB.
  3. VM2 = 132 − 52 = 169 − 25 = 144 — Pythagoras' theorem
  4. VM = √144 = 12 cm
  5. Check: 122 = 144 and 144 + 25 = 169 = 132 ✓
