Question 3  [3 marks]
(a)Write down the letter of the face that is opposite face A.
  1. Squares that share an edge in the net become faces that meet along an edge of the cube, so they can never be opposite. — check pairs that are further apart
  2. Fold the net keeping square A flat as the top of the cube: B folds down to become the right-hand face and C folds round to become the front face. — each square turns through 90° along the shared edge
  3. D is joined to C and folds underneath, so the face opposite A is D — top and bottom
(b)Write down the letter of the face that is opposite face B.
  1. Continuing the fold, E is joined to D and becomes the left-hand face of the cube.
  2. B is the right-hand face, so the face opposite B is E — left and right
(c)Write down the letter of the face that is opposite face C.
  1. Finally F is joined to E and becomes the back face.
  2. C is the front face, so the face opposite C is F — front and back
  3. Check: the three pairs A–D, B–E and C–F use all six faces exactly once ✓

Question 4  [4 marks]
(a)Write down the length of the third side of the triangle.
  1. The triangle is right-angled with shorter sides 3 cm and 4 cm.
  2. Third side = √(32 + 42) = √25 = 5 cm — Pythagoras' theorem
(b)Work out the total surface area of the prism.
  1. The net is two triangles and three rectangles.
  2. Two triangles = 2 × 1⁄2 × 3 × 4 = 12 cm2
  3. Three rectangles = (3 + 4 + 5) × 10 = 12 × 10 = 120 cm2 — the perimeter of the cross-section × the length
  4. Surface area = 12 + 120 = 132 cm2
(c)Calculate the volume of the prism.

Question 7  [4 marks]
(a)Write down the mathematical name of the solid.
  1. The two hexagons are the identical parallel ends and the six rectangles wrap around them.
  2. The solid is a hexagonal prism — a prism with a hexagonal cross-section
(b)Write down the number of vertices of the solid.
  1. Each hexagon has 6 corners and there are two hexagonal ends.
  2. Number of vertices V = 2 × 6 = 12
(c)Write down the number of edges of the solid.
  1. 6 edges on each hexagon and 6 edges joining the two hexagons.
  2. Number of edges E = 6 + 6 + 6 = 18
(d)Show that F + V − E = 2 for this solid, where F, V and E are the numbers of faces, vertices and edges.
  1. F = 2 hexagons + 6 rectangles = 8.
  2. F + V − E = 8 + 12 − 18 = 2 ✓ — Euler's formula for a solid

Question 9  [5 marks]

  4. x⁄360 × 20π = 12π, so x⁄360 = 12⁄20 = 0.6
  5. x = 0.6 × 360 = 216°

Question 10  [6 marks]
(a)Show that the total surface area of the box is 14x2 cm2.
  1. The net has two squares of side x and four rectangles x by 3x.
  2. Surface area = 2 × x2 + 4 × (x × 3x)
  3. = 2x2 + 12x2 — 4 × 3x2 = 12x2
  4. Surface area = 14x2 cm2
(b)The total surface area of the box is 350 cm2.
Find the value of x.
  1. 14x2 = 350
  2. x2 = 350⁄14 = 25 — divide both sides by 14
  3. x = 5 — x is a length, so take the positive root
(c)Calculate the volume of the box.
  1. The box measures 5 cm by 5 cm by 15 cm. — 3x = 15
  2. Volume = 5 × 5 × 15
  3. Volume = 375 cm3
