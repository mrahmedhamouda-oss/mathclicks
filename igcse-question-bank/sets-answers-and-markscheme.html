Question 9  [5 marks]
(a)Work out the number of people who drank tea only.
  1. Start from the centre: 8 drank all three. — always fill a Venn diagram inwards‑out
  2. Tea and coffee only = 15 − 8 = 7. — the 15 includes the 8 in the centre
  3. Tea and juice only = 10 − 8 = 2.
  4. Tea only = 30 − (7 + 2 + 8) = 30 − 17 = 13 people

  1. Coffee and juice only = 12 − 8 = 4, coffee only = 28 − (7 + 4 + 8) = 9, juice only = 20 − (2 + 4 + 8) = 6.

  1. Exactly two drinks means the three regions where just two circles overlap.
  2. 7 + 4 + 2 = 13 people. — these do not include the 8 in the centre
  3. Probability = 13⁄50 — = 0.26

Question 10  [6 marks]
(a)Show that the four regions of the Venn diagram give the equation 56 + x = 60, and find the value of x.
  1. A only = 34 − x, both = x, B only = 27 − x, outside = 2x − 5.
  2. (34 − x) + x + (27 − x) + (2x − 5) = 60 — the four regions make up the whole universal set
  3. 34 + 27 − 5 = 56 and −x + x − x + 2x = x, so 56 + x = 60. — collect like terms
  4. x = 4 — subtract 56 from both sides
  5. Check: 30 + 4 + 23 + 3 = 60 ✓
(b)Find n(A′ ∩ B).
  1. A′ ∩ B is the part of B outside A.
  2. 27 − 4 = 23
(c)An element of ξ is chosen at random.
Find the probability that it belongs to exactly one of the sets A and B.
  1. Exactly one set means A only or B only.
  2. (34 − 4) + (27 − 4) = 30 + 23 = 53.
  3. Probability = 53⁄60 — = 0.883 (3 s.f.)
