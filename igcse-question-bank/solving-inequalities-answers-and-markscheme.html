Question 6  [4 marks]
(a)Write down an inequality in n to show this information.
  1. Total spent = 12 for entry + 7 for each of the n books.
  2. Total = 7n + 12
  3. ‘Cannot spend more than $60’ means the total is at most 60.
  4. 7n + 12 ≤ 60
(b)Work out the greatest number of books Amara can buy.
  1. 7n + 12 ≤ 60
  2. 7n ≤ 48 — subtract 12 from both sides
  3. n ≤ 6.857… — divide both sides by 7
  4. n must be a whole number of books, so the greatest value is 6 books — 6.857… is rounded down, not to the nearest integer
  5. Check: 6 books cost 7 × 6 + 12 = $54 ✓, but 7 books cost $61 ✗

Question 10  [6 marks]
(a)Factorise x2 − 2x − 15.
  1. Two numbers with product −15 and sum −2 are −5 and +3.
  2. (x − 5)(x + 3)
(b)Solve the inequality x2 < 2x + 15.
  1. x2 < 2x + 15
  2. x2 − 2x − 15 < 0 — collect everything on one side, leaving 0 on the other
  3. (x − 5)(x + 3) < 0 — using part (a)
  4. Critical values are x = 5 and x = −3.
  5. The curve y = x2 − 2x − 15 is a positive parabola, so it is below the axis between the roots.
  6. −3 < x < 5
  7. Check: x = 0 gives 0 < 15 ✓, and x = 6 gives 36 < 27 ✗
(c)Solve the inequality 2x2 ≥ 50.
  1. 2x2 ≥ 50
  2. x2 ≥ 25 — divide both sides by 2
  3. The critical values are x = 5 and x = −5. — both square roots must be considered
  4. A positive parabola is above 25 outside the two critical values.
  5. x ≤ −5  or  x ≥ 5
  6. Check: x = −6 gives 2 × 36 = 72 ≥ 50 ✓
