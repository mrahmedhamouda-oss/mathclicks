A map is drawn to a scale of 1 : 50 000.

A road atlas uses a scale of 1 : 200 000.

(a)
Two petrol stations are 6.5 cm apart on the map. Work out the actual distance between them, in kilometres.
km[2]
(b)

3

Priya draws a plan of her school hall.

(a)
The hall is 26 m long. Work out the length of the hall on the plan.
cm[2]
(b)
A door is 1.5 cm wide on the plan. Write down the actual width of the door.
m[1]

(a)
Calculate the actual length of the track. Give your answer in kilometres.
km[2]
(b)

minutes[2]
