Question 1  [3 marks]

  1. 3x + y = 11   (1)
    x − y = 1   (2) — label the equations
  2. (1) + (2):   4x = 12 — the coefficients of y are +1 and −1, so adding eliminates y
  3. x = 12 ÷ 4 = 3
  4. Substitute into (2):   3 − y = 1, so y = 2
  5. x = 3 and y = 2
  6. Check in (1): 3 × 3 + 2 = 11 ✓ — always check in the equation you did not use
