Question 1  [3 marks]
Solve the simultaneous equations.
  1. 3x + y = 11   (1)
    x − y = 1   (2) — label the equations
  2. (1) + (2):   4x = 12 — the coefficients of y are +1 and −1, so adding eliminates y
  3. x = 12 ÷ 4 = 3
  4. Substitute into (2):   3 − y = 1, so y = 2
  5. x = 3 and y = 2
  6. Check in (1): 3 × 3 + 2 = 11 ✓ — always check in the equation you did not use

AnswerMarksPartial marks / notes

A1 for x = 3
A1 for y = 2
If M0, SC1 for a correct pair of values with no working
Question 2  [3 marks]
Solve the simultaneous equations.
  1. 4x + 3y = 18   (1)
    x + 3y = 9   (2)
  2. (1) − (2):   3x = 9 — the y terms are identical, so subtracting eliminates y
  3. x = 3
  4. Substitute into (2):   3 + 3y = 9, so 3y = 6 and y = 2
  5. x = 3 and y = 2
  6. Check in (1): 4 × 3 + 3 × 2 = 12 + 6 = 18 ✓

Question 3  [3 marks]
Solve the simultaneous equations.
  1. y = 2x − 1   (1)
    3x + y = 14   (2)
  2. Substitute (1) into (2):   3x + (2x − 1) = 14 — (1) already gives y in terms of x, so substitution is quickest
  3. 5x − 1 = 14 — collect like terms
  4. 5x = 15, so x = 3
  5. y = 2 × 3 − 1 = 5 — substitute back into (1)
  6. x = 3 and y = 5
  7. Check in (2): 3 × 3 + 5 = 14 ✓

    3x − 2y = −2   (2)

  3. (2) × 5:   15x − 10y = −10   (4)

  5. Substitute into (1):   4 + 5y = 24, so 5y = 20 and y = 4
  6. x = 2 and y = 4

Question 8  [4 marks]
(a)Show that 2m + c = 7.
  1. The line passes through (2, 7), so x = 2 and y = 7 fit y = mx + c.
  2. 7 = m × 2 + c, so 2m + c = 7 — as required
(b)Write down a second equation in m and c, and solve the two equations to find m and c.
  1. The line also passes through (5, 19), so 5m + c = 19.
  2. 2m + c = 7   (1)
    5m + c = 19   (2)
  3. (2) − (1):   3m = 12, so m = 4 — the c terms are identical, so subtracting eliminates c
  4. Substitute into (1):   8 + c = 7, so c = −1
  5. m = 4 and c = −1, so the line is y = 4x − 1
  6. Check: at x = 5, y = 4 × 5 − 1 = 19 ✓

Question 9  [5 marks]
(a)Show that x2 + x − 6 = 0.
  1. At a point of intersection the two expressions for y are equal.
  2. x2 + 3x = 2x + 6
  3. x2 + 3x − 2x − 6 = 0 — subtract 2x + 6 from both sides
  4. x2 + x − 6 = 0 — as required
(b)Solve the simultaneous equations.
  1. Factorise: two numbers with product −6 and sum +1 are +3 and −2.
  2. (x + 3)(x − 2) = 0
  3. x = −3  or  x = 2
  4. When x = −3: y = 2 × (−3) + 6 = 0 — use the simpler equation
  5. When x = 2: y = 2 × 2 + 6 = 10
  6. x = −3, y = 0  and  x = 2, y = 10
  7. Check in the curve: (−3)2 + 3 × (−3) = 0 ✓ and 22 + 3 × 2 = 10 ✓

Question 10  [6 marks]
(a)Show that x2 + x − 12 = 0.
  1. Substitute y = x + 1 into x2 + y2 = 25.
  2. x2 + (x + 1)2 = 25
  3. x2 + x2 + 2x + 1 = 25 — expand (x + 1)2 = x2 + 2x + 1
  4. 2x2 + 2x − 24 = 0
  5. x2 + x − 12 = 0 — divide every term by 2
(b)Find the coordinates of A and of B.
  1. Factorise: (x + 4)(x − 3) = 0 — product −12, sum +1
  2. x = −4  or  x = 3
  3. When x = −4: y = −4 + 1 = −3
  4. When x = 3: y = 3 + 1 = 4
  5. A(−4, −3) and B(3, 4)
  6. Check: (−4)2 + (−3)2 = 16 + 9 = 25 ✓
(c)Calculate the length of AB.
Give your answer correct to 3 significant figures.
  1. Horizontal step = 3 − (−4) = 7 and vertical step = 4 − (−3) = 7.
  2. AB2 = 72 + 72 = 98 — Pythagoras' theorem
  3. AB = √98 = 7√2 = 9.8994…
  4. AB = 9.90 (3 s.f.)
