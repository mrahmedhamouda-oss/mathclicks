Question 8  [4 marks]

  1. HCF × LCM = product of the two numbers.

  5. Check: HCF(18, 30) = 6 and LCM(18, 30) = 90 ✓

(a)Two prime numbers have a sum of 45.

  1. 45 is odd, so one of the two primes must be even and the other odd. — odd + odd = even, so two odd primes cannot add to 45

  3. The primes are 13 and 17 — 13 × 17 = 221 ✓

Question 23  [5 marks]
(a)The highest common factor of x and 60 is 12.
Find all the possible values of x.
  1. x must be a multiple of 12: 12, 24, 36, 48, 60, 72, 84, 96. — the HCF divides x
  2. 60 = 12 × 5. If x is also a multiple of 5 the HCF would be 60, so 60 must be removed. — HCF(60, 60) = 60, not 12
  3. Every other multiple of 12 has HCF 12 with 60: for example HCF(84, 60) = 12 because 84 = 12 × 7 and 7 has no factor in common with 5.
  4. x = 12, 24, 36, 48, 72, 84 or 96
(b)The lowest common multiple of y and 60 is 180.
Find all the possible values of y.
  1. y must be a factor of 180 = 22 × 32 × 5, and 60 = 22 × 3 × 5 provides only one 3, so y must contain 32 = 9. — the LCM must reach 32
  2. The factors of 180 that are multiples of 9 and less than 100 are 9, 18, 36, 45, 90. — 180 itself is not less than 100
  3. y = 9, 18, 36, 45 or 90 — check: LCM(45, 60) = 180 ✓

Question 24  [5 marks]
(a)A positive integer has exactly three factors.
Explain why the integer must be the square of a prime number, and write down the largest such integer that is less than 200.
  1. Factors come in pairs d and n ÷ d, so a number has an odd number of factors only when one factor pairs with itself, i.e. the number is a square.
  2. If n = p2 the factors are 1, p and p2; if p were not prime it would bring extra factors. So n is the square of a prime.
  3. Squares of primes: 4, 9, 25, 49, 121, 169, 289, … The largest below 200 is 169 — 169 = 132

  3. 25 × 3 = 96,  23 × 32 = 72,  22 × 33 = 108,  2 × 35 = 486
