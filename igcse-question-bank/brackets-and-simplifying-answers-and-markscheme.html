Question 1  [2 marks]
(a)9m + 4k − 5m + k
  1. Group the m terms and the k terms: 9m − 5m and 4k + k. — only like terms can be added or subtracted
  2. 9m − 5m = 4m and 4k + k = 5k
  3. Answer: 4m + 5k
(b)5x2 + 3x − 2x2 + x
  1. x2 terms: 5x2 − 2x2 = 3x2 — x2 and x are not like terms, so they stay separate
  2. x terms: 3x + x = 4x
  3. Answer: 3x2 + 4x

Question 2  [3 marks]
(a)Expand 6(3x − 4).
  1. Multiply each term inside the bracket by 6.
  2. 6 × 3x = 18x and 6 × (−4) = −24
  3. Answer: 18x − 24
(b)Expand and simplify 4(2y + 3) + 5(y − 1).
  1. 4(2y + 3) = 8y + 12 — expand the first bracket
  2. 5(y − 1) = 5y − 5 — expand the second bracket
  3. 8y + 12 + 5y − 5 = 13y + 7 — collect like terms
  4. Answer: 13y + 7

Question 3  [3 marks]
(a)Expand and simplify 7(x + 4) − 3(2x − 5).
  1. 7(x + 4) = 7x + 28
  2. −3(2x − 5) = −6x + 15 — the −3 multiplies both terms; −3 × −5 = +15
  3. 7x + 28 − 6x + 15 = x + 43 — collect like terms
  4. Answer: x + 43
(b)Expand −2(3w − 5).
  1. −2 × 3w = −6w

Question 4  [4 marks]

  2. x × x = x2,   x × (−3) = −3x,   7 × x = 7x,   7 × (−3) = −21

  4. Answer: x2 + 4x − 21 — collect the two x terms

Question 5  [4 marks]
(a)Show that the perimeter of the rectangle is (6x + 16) cm.
  1. Perimeter = 2 × length + 2 × width — a rectangle has two pairs of equal sides
  2. = 2(2x + 5) + 2(x + 3)
  3. = 4x + 10 + 2x + 6 — expand both brackets
  4. = 6x + 16 as required — collect like terms

  1. Area = length × width = (2x + 5)(x + 3)

  3. Area = 2x2 + 11x + 15 cm2 — collect like terms

Question 6  [4 marks]
(a)(3n − 4)2
  1. (3n − 4)2 means (3n − 4)(3n − 4). — squaring is not done term by term
  2. 3n × 3n = 9n2,   3n × (−4) = −12n,   −4 × 3n = −12n,   −4 × (−4) = +16
  3. Answer: 9n2 − 24n + 16 — collect the two middle terms
(b)(5p + 2)(5p − 2)
  1. 5p × 5p = 25p2,   5p × (−2) = −10p,   2 × 5p = +10p,   2 × (−2) = −4
  2. The two middle terms cancel: −10p + 10p = 0. — this is the difference of two squares
  3. Answer: 25p2 − 4

Question 9  [5 marks]
(a)Expand and simplify (2x − 1)(x + 3)(x − 2).
  1. Expand the first two brackets: (2x − 1)(x + 3) = 2x2 + 6x − x − 3 = 2x2 + 5x − 3 — multiply two brackets at a time
  2. Now multiply by (x − 2):
  3. (2x2 + 5x − 3)(x − 2) = 2x3 + 5x2 − 3x − 4x2 − 10x + 6 — every term of the quadratic times each term of the bracket
  4. Answer: 2x3 + x2 − 13x + 6 — 5x2 − 4x2 = x2 and −3x − 10x = −13x
(b)Write down the value of the product when x = 2. Give a reason for your answer.
  1. When x = 2 the third bracket is 2 − 2 = 0.
  2. Anything multiplied by 0 is 0, so the product is 0. — one factor is zero, so the whole product is zero
  3. Check with the expansion: 2(8) + 4 − 26 + 6 = 16 + 4 − 26 + 6 = 0 ✓
(c)Write down the coefficient of x2 in your expansion.
  1. Read the coefficient of x2 from 2x3 + x2 − 13x + 6.
  2. The coefficient is 1 — x2 means 1x2

Question 10  [6 marks]
(a)Show that the area of the garden that is not covered by the pond is (x2 + 15x + 20) m2.
  1. Area of garden = (2x + 7)(x + 3)
  2. = 2x2 + 6x + 7x + 21 = 2x2 + 13x + 21
  3. Area of pond = (x − 1)2 = x2 − 2x + 1 — (x − 1)(x − 1), not x2 − 1
  4. Uncovered area = (2x2 + 13x + 21) − (x2 − 2x + 1) — subtract the whole of the second bracket
  5. = 2x2 + 13x + 21 − x2 + 2x − 1
  6. = x2 + 15x + 20 as required
(b)The perimeter of the garden is 50 m.
Work out the value of x.
  1. Perimeter = 2(2x + 7) + 2(x + 3) = 4x + 14 + 2x + 6 = 6x + 20
  2. 6x + 20 = 50 — the perimeter is given as 50 m
  3. 6x = 30 — subtract 20 from both sides
  4. x = 5 — divide both sides by 6
  5. Check: the sides are 17 m and 8 m, and 2(17 + 8) = 50 ✓
(c)Work out the area of the garden that is not covered by the pond.
  1. Substitute x = 5 into x2 + 15x + 20.
  2. = 25 + 75 + 20
  3. Uncovered area = 120 m2 — check: garden 17 × 8 = 136 and pond 4 × 4 = 16, and 136 − 16 = 120 ✓
