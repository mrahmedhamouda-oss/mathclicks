Question 9  [5 marks]
(a)Make x the subject of the formula.
  1. y = (3x + 1)⁄(x − 2)
  2. y(x − 2) = 3x + 1 — multiply both sides by (x − 2) to clear the fraction
  3. xy − 2y = 3x + 1 — expand the bracket
  4. xy − 3x = 2y + 1 — collect the terms containing x on one side
  5. x(y − 3) = 2y + 1 — factorise the left-hand side
  6. x = (2y + 1)⁄(y − 3) — divide by (y − 3)
  7. Check: x = 5.5 gives y = 17.5⁄3.5 = 5, and (10 + 1)⁄(5 − 3) = 5.5 ✓
(b)Write down the one value of y that this formula can never take. Give a reason for your answer.
  1. In the rearranged formula the denominator is y − 3.
  2. y = 3 would make that denominator 0, and division by 0 is not defined, so y can never equal 3. — equivalently, 3x + 1 = 3(x − 2) has no solution

Question 10  [6 marks]
(a)Show that a = b(t2 + 3)⁄(t2 − 1).
