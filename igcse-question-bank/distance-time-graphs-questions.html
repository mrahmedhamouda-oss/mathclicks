Priya walks from her home to a park, waits there, and then walks straight home again.
The travel graph shows her journey.

(a)
Write down how long Priya waits at the park.
minutes[1]
(b)
Work out her walking speed for the first 30 minutes.
Give your answer in km/h.
km/h[1]

km/h[2]

(a)

seconds[1]

The travel graph shows both journeys.

(a)
Write down the speed of the bus.
km/h[1]
(b)
Write down the speed of the motorcycle.
km/h[1]
(c)

km/h[2]

(a)

km/h[2]
