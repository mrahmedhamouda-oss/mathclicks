Question 9  [5 marks]
(a)Find the equation of the straight line that must be drawn on the grid to solve the equation x³ − 3x − 2 = 0.
  1. The curve is y = x³ − 5x + 1 and the line is y = mx + c.

  3. Subtract: (x³ − 5x + 1) − (x³ − 3x − 2) = −2x + 3

  2. The line is a tangent to the curve at x = −1, so that solution is repeated. — the factorised form (x + 1)²(x − 2) = 0 shows the repeated root
