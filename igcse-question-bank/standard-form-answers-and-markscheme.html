Question 1  [2 marks]
(a)4 800 000
  1. Standard form is a × 10n with 1 ≤ a < 10.
  2. The digits give a = 4.8; moving the point from 4.8 to 4 800 000 is 6 places to the right. — each place is one multiplication by 10
  3. 4 800 000 = 4.8 × 106
(b)0.000 062
  1. The digits give a = 6.2; 0.000 062 is smaller than 1, so the index is negative.
  2. Moving the point from 6.2 to 0.000 062 is 5 places to the left.
  3. 0.000 062 = 6.2 × 10−5

Question 2  [3 marks]
(a)3.06 × 105 as an ordinary number
  1. The index 5 moves the decimal point 5 places to the right. — multiplying by 105
  2. 3.06 → 306 000, filling the empty places with zeros.
  3. 3.06 × 105 = 306 000
(b)9.4 × 10−4 as an ordinary number
  1. The index −4 moves the decimal point 4 places to the left. — dividing by 104
  2. 9.4 → 0.000 94
  3. 9.4 × 10−4 = 0.000 94
(c)0.000 000 45 in standard form
  1. The first non‑zero digit is 4, so a = 4.5.
  2. 0.000 000 45 is 4.5 divided by 107. — 7 places from 4.5 to 0.000 000 45
  3. 0.000 000 45 = 4.5 × 10−7

Question 4  [3 marks]
(a)(2.5 × 106) × (4 × 10−2)
  1. Multiply the numbers and add the indices. — 10m × 10n = 10m+n
  2. 2.5 × 4 = 10   and   106 × 10−2 = 104
  3. 10 × 104 = 1 × 105 — 10 is not between 1 and 10, so adjust the index
  4. Check: 2 500 000 × 0.04 = 100 000 ✓
(b)(9.6 × 10−4) ÷ (1.6 × 105)
  1. Divide the numbers and subtract the indices. — 10m ÷ 10n = 10m−n
  2. 9.6 ÷ 1.6 = 6   and   10−4 ÷ 105 = 10−9
  3. Answer = 6 × 10−9
(c)(3 × 104)3
  1. Cube the number and multiply the index by 3. — (a × 10n)3 = a3 × 103n
  2. 33 = 27   and   (104)3 = 1012
  3. 27 × 1012 = 2.7 × 1013

Question 5  [4 marks]
(a)(7.4 × 105) + (6.8 × 104)
  1. The indices are different, so the powers of 10 cannot simply be added.
  2. 7.4 × 105 = 740 000   and   6.8 × 104 = 68 000 — write both as ordinary numbers
  3. 740 000 + 68 000 = 808 000
  4. Answer = 8.08 × 105

  3. 0.000 52 − 0.000 089 = 0.000 431
  4. Answer = 4.31 × 10−4

Question 6  [4 marks]
(a)Calculate the mass of the rice in one sack.
  1. Mass = mass of one grain × number of grains
  2. = (2.9 × 10−5) × (4.5 × 105)
  3. 2.9 × 4.5 = 13.05   and   10−5 × 105 = 100 = 1
  4. Mass = 13.05 kg — = 1.305 × 101 kg in standard form
(b)A lorry carries 8.4 × 103 of these sacks.
Calculate the total mass of rice on the lorry.
Give your answer in standard form, correct to 3 significant figures.
  1. Total mass = 13.05 × (8.4 × 103) — one sack is 13.05 kg
  2. 13.05 × 8.4 = 109.62, so the total is 109.62 × 103 = 109 620 kg
  3. 109 620 = 1.0962 × 105
  4. Total mass = 1.10 × 105 kg (3 s.f.)

Question 7  [4 marks]
(a)Calculate the number of people per square kilometre.
Give your answer correct to 3 significant figures.
  1. People per km2 = population ÷ area
  2. = (3.42 × 107) ÷ (5.6 × 105)
  3. 3.42 ÷ 5.6 = 0.610714…   and   107 ÷ 105 = 102
  4. 0.610714… × 100 = 61.0714…
  5. Number of people per km2 = 61.1 (3 s.f.)
(b)The population then increases by 12%.
Calculate the new population, giving your answer in standard form correct to 3 significant figures.
  1. An increase of 12% means multiplying by 1.12. — 100% + 12% = 112%
  2. New population = (3.42 × 107) × 1.12
  3. 3.42 × 1.12 = 3.8304, so the new population is 3.8304 × 107 = 38 304 000
  4. New population = 3.83 × 107 (3 s.f.)

Question 8  [4 marks]
(a)Calculate the distance light travels in 6.5 × 102 seconds.
Give your answer in standard form.
  1. Distance = speed × time
  2. = (3 × 108) × (6.5 × 102)
  3. 3 × 6.5 = 19.5   and   108 × 102 = 1010
  4. 19.5 × 1010 = 1.95 × 1011 m — 19.5 is not between 1 and 10, so adjust
(b)The star Vela is 4.1 × 1016 metres from Earth.
Calculate the time light takes to travel from Vela to Earth.
Give your answer in standard form, correct to 3 significant figures.
  1. Time = distance ÷ speed
  2. = (4.1 × 1016) ÷ (3 × 108)
  3. 4.1 ÷ 3 = 1.3666…   and   1016 ÷ 108 = 108
  4. Time = 1.37 × 108 s (3 s.f.)

Question 9  [5 marks]
(a)Work out pq, giving your answer in standard form.
  1. pq = (6.4 × 105) × (8 × 10−3)
  2. 6.4 × 8 = 51.2   and   105 × 10−3 = 102 — add the indices
  3. 51.2 × 102 = 5120 = 5.12 × 103
(b)Work out p ÷ q, giving your answer in standard form.
  1. p ÷ q = (6.4 × 105) ÷ (8 × 10−3)
  2. 6.4 ÷ 8 = 0.8   and   105 ÷ 10−3 = 105−(−3) = 108 — subtract the indices
  3. 0.8 × 108 = 8 × 107 — 0.8 is less than 1, so reduce the index by 1
(c)Work out √(p ÷ q), giving your answer in standard form correct to 3 significant figures.
  1. √(8 × 107) = √(80 × 106) = √80 × 103 — write the index as an even number so the root is exact
  2. √80 = 8.9442…, so the value is 8944.27…
  3. √(p ÷ q) = 8.94 × 103 (3 s.f.)

Question 10  [6 marks]
(a)Show that (4 × 10n) × (2.5 × 10n+1) = 102n+2.
  1. Multiply the numbers and add the indices.
  2. 4 × 2.5 = 10
  3. 10n × 10n+1 = 102n+1 — add the indices
  4. Product = 10 × 102n+1 = 101 × 102n+1 = 102n+2  as required
(b)Hence find the value of n for which (4 × 10n) × (2.5 × 10n+1) = 1 × 1012.
  1. From part (a) the product is 102n+2, and 1 × 1012 = 1012.
  2. So 2n + 2 = 12 — the bases are equal, so the indices are equal
  3. 2n = 10, so n = 5
  4. Check: (4 × 105) × (2.5 × 106) = 10 × 1011 = 1012 ✓
(c)Work out (1.2 × 10−4)2 ÷ (3 × 10−5), giving your answer in standard form.
  1. Square the first bracket: (1.2)2 = 1.44 and (10−4)2 = 10−8. — multiply the index by 2
  2. (1.2 × 10−4)2 = 1.44 × 10−8
  3. 1.44 ÷ 3 = 0.48   and   10−8 ÷ 10−5 = 10−3 — subtract the indices
  4. 0.48 × 10−3 = 4.8 × 10−4
