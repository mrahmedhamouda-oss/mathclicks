Question 10  [6 marks]
(a)Show that (4 × 10n) × (2.5 × 10n+1) = 102n+2.
  1. Multiply the numbers and add the indices.
  2. 4 × 2.5 = 10
  3. 10n × 10n+1 = 102n+1 — add the indices
  4. Product = 10 × 102n+1 = 101 × 102n+1 = 102n+2  as required
(b)Hence find the value of n for which (4 × 10n) × (2.5 × 10n+1) = 1 × 1012.
  1. From part (a) the product is 102n+2, and 1 × 1012 = 1012.
  2. So 2n + 2 = 12 — the bases are equal, so the indices are equal
  3. 2n = 10, so n = 5
  4. Check: (4 × 105) × (2.5 × 106) = 10 × 1011 = 1012 ✓
(c)Work out (1.2 × 10−4)2 ÷ (3 × 10−5), giving your answer in standard form.
  1. Square the first bracket: (1.2)2 = 1.44 and (10−4)2 = 10−8. — multiply the index by 2
  2. (1.2 × 10−4)2 = 1.44 × 10−8
  3. 1.44 ÷ 3 = 0.48   and   10−8 ÷ 10−5 = 10−3 — subtract the indices
  4. 0.48 × 10−3 = 4.8 × 10−4
