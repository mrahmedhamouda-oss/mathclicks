Question 10  [6 marks]
(a)Write down the bearing of P from Q.
  1. The bearing of P from Q is the back bearing of 118°.
  2. Bearing = 118 + 180 = 298° — the two directions along PQ differ by 180°
(b)Calculate angle PQR.
  1. At Q, the direction of P is 298° and the direction of R is 226°.
  2. Angle PQR = 298 − 226 = 72° — both bearings are measured clockwise from the same north line at Q
(c)Calculate the bearing of P from R.
  1. PQ = QR, so triangle PQR is isosceles with angle QRP = angle QPR. — base angles of an isosceles triangle are equal
  2. Angle QRP = (180 − 72) ÷ 2 = 54° — angle sum of a triangle is 180°
  3. Bearing of Q from R = 226 − 180 = 046° — back bearing of QR
  4. P lies 54° anticlockwise from Q as seen from R, so the bearing is 046 − 54 = −8°.
  5. Bearing of P from R = 360 − 8 = 352° — add 360° to give a three-figure bearing
