Question 1  [2 marks]
(a)Find angle AOD.
  1. COD is a straight line, so angles AOC and AOD together make a half turn.
  2. Angle AOD = 180 − 116 = 64° — angles on a straight line add up to 180°
(b)Write down angle BOD.
  1. Angle BOD is directly across the crossing point from angle AOC.
  2. Angle BOD = 116° — vertically opposite angles are equal
  3. Check: 116 + 64 + 116 + 64 = 360 ✓ — angles at a point add up to 360°

Question 2  [2 marks]
(a)Find angle PQR.
  1. PQ = PR, so triangle PQR is isosceles and angle PQR = angle PRQ. — base angles of an isosceles triangle are equal
  2. 2 × angle PQR = 180 − 46 = 134 — angle sum of a triangle is 180°
  3. Angle PQR = 134 ÷ 2 = 67°
(b)Find angle PRS.
  1. Angle PRQ = 67° as well. — the two base angles are equal
  2. QRS is a straight line, so angle PRS = 180 − 67 = 113° — angles on a straight line add up to 180°

Question 8  [4 marks]
(a)Write down the angle between the two hands of the clock at 3:00.
  1. At 3:00 the minute hand points at 12 and the hour hand points at 3.
  2. That is 3 of the 12 equal divisions, so the angle is 3 × 30 = 90° — a complete turn of 360° is divided into 12 equal parts of 30°
(b)Calculate the value of x.
  1. Minute hand: 20 minutes past the hour is 20⁄60 of a turn.
  2. Angle turned by the minute hand from 12 = 20⁄60 × 360 = 120°
  3. Hour hand: the time is 41⁄3 hours after 12. — the hour hand keeps moving between the numbers
  4. Angle turned by the hour hand from 12 = 41⁄3 × 30 = 130° — the hour hand turns 30° each hour
  5. x = 130 − 120 = 10 — subtract the two angles measured from 12

Question 9  [5 marks]
(a)Find angle ABC.
  1. AB = AC, so triangle ABC is isosceles with angle ABC = angle ACB. — base angles of an isosceles triangle are equal
  2. Angle ABC = (180 − 40) ÷ 2 = 70° — angle sum of a triangle is 180°
(b)Find angle BDC, giving a reason for your answer.
  1. Angle BCD is the same angle as angle ACB = 70°, since D lies on AC.
  2. BD = BC, so triangle BDC is isosceles and angle BDC = angle BCD. — base angles of an isosceles triangle are equal
  3. Angle BDC = 70°
(c)Find angle DBC.
  1. In triangle BDC: angle BDC = angle BCD = 70°.
  2. Angle DBC = 180 − 70 − 70 = 40° — angle sum of a triangle is 180°
(d)Find angle ABD.
  1. Angle ABC is split by BD into angle ABD and angle DBC.
  2. Angle ABD = 70 − 40 = 30°
  3. Check: in triangle ABD, 40 + 30 + angle ADB = 180 gives angle ADB = 110°, and 110 + 70 = 180 along the straight line ADC ✓

Question 10  [6 marks]
(a)Write down the bearing of P from Q.
  1. The bearing of P from Q is the back bearing of 118°.
  2. Bearing = 118 + 180 = 298° — the two directions along PQ differ by 180°
(b)Calculate angle PQR.
  1. At Q, the direction of P is 298° and the direction of R is 226°.
  2. Angle PQR = 298 − 226 = 72° — both bearings are measured clockwise from the same north line at Q
(c)Calculate the bearing of P from R.
  1. PQ = QR, so triangle PQR is isosceles with angle QRP = angle QPR. — base angles of an isosceles triangle are equal
  2. Angle QRP = (180 − 72) ÷ 2 = 54° — angle sum of a triangle is 180°
  3. Bearing of Q from R = 226 − 180 = 046° — back bearing of QR
  4. P lies 54° anticlockwise from Q as seen from R, so the bearing is 046 − 54 = −8°.
  5. Bearing of P from R = 360 − 8 = 352° — add 360° to give a three-figure bearing
