  3. 3x = 21 — add 1 to both sides
  4. x = 7 — divide both sides by 3
  5. Check: (3 × 7 − 1) ÷ 4 = 20 ÷ 4 = 5 ✓

Question 6  [4 marks]

Question 8  [4 marks]
(a)Show that 6x = 180.
  1. (2x + 10) + (3x − 15) + (x + 5) = 180 — the angles of a triangle add up to 180°
  2. x terms: 2x + 3x + x = 6x; numbers: 10 − 15 + 5 = 0 — collect like terms
  3. So 6x = 180, as required
(b)Solve to find the value of x.
  1. 6x = 180
  2. x = 180 ÷ 6 = 30 — divide both sides by 6
(c)Work out the size of the largest angle of the triangle.
  1. 2x + 10 = 70, 3x − 15 = 75, x + 5 = 35 — substitute x = 30 into each expression
  2. The largest of 70, 75 and 35 is 75°
  3. Check: 70 + 75 + 35 = 180 ✓

Question 9  [5 marks]
(a)Write down, in terms of x, the age of Sami and the age of their mother.
  1. Sami is 6 years older than Layla, so Sami is (x + 6) years old.
  2. Their mother is three times Layla's age, so she is 3x years old.
  3. Ages: x + 6 and 3x
(b)Write down an equation in x and show that it simplifies to 5x = 60.
  1. x + (x + 6) + 3x = 66 — the three ages add up to 66
  2. x + x + 3x = 5x and the constant 6 moves to the right — collect like terms
  3. 5x + 6 = 66, so 5x = 60, as required — subtract 6 from both sides
(c)Solve the equation to find Layla's age.
  1. 5x = 60
  2. x = 60 ÷ 5 = 12 — divide both sides by 5
  3. Layla is 12 years old.

  1. Sami is x + 6 = 12 + 6 years old.
  2. Sami is 18 years old.
  3. Check: 12 + 18 + 36 = 66 ✓ — the mother is 3 × 12 = 36

Question 10  [6 marks]
(a)2x + 1⁄5 − x − 3⁄2 = 1
  1. 2x + 1⁄5 − x − 3⁄2 = 1
  2. 2(2x + 1) − 5(x − 3) = 10 — multiply every term by 10, the lowest common multiple of 5 and 2
  3. 4x + 2 − 5x + 15 = 10 — expand; note −5 × (−3) = +15
  4. −x + 17 = 10 — collect like terms
  5. −x = −7, so x = 7
  6. Check: 15⁄5 − 4⁄2 = 3 − 2 = 1 ✓
(b)x + 4⁄3 = 2x − 1⁄5
  1. x + 4⁄3 = 2x − 1⁄5
  2. 5(x + 4) = 3(2x − 1) — cross‑multiply (multiply both sides by 15)
  3. 5x + 20 = 6x − 3 — expand both brackets
  4. 23 = x, so x = 23 — subtract 5x from both sides and add 3
  5. Check: 27⁄3 = 9 and 45⁄5 = 9 ✓
