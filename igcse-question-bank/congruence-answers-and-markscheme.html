Question 3  [3 marks]
(a)Show that triangle ABM is congruent to triangle ACM, stating which condition of congruency you use.
  1. AB = AC — given, the triangle is isosceles
  2. BM = CM — M is the midpoint of BC
  3. AM = AM — AM is common to both triangles
  4. Three pairs of equal sides, so triangle ABM is congruent to triangle ACM (SSS)
(b)Write down the size of angle AMB. Give a reason for your answer.
  1. Angle AMB = angle AMC — corresponding angles of the congruent triangles
  2. Angle AMB + angle AMC = 180° — BMC is a straight line
  3. Angle AMB = 180 ÷ 2 = 90° — so AM is perpendicular to BC

Question 7  [4 marks]
(a)Show that triangle ABC is congruent to triangle CDA. Give a reason for each statement you make.
  1. Angle BAC = angle DCA — alternate angles, because AB is parallel to DC
  2. AC = CA — AC is a side of both triangles
  3. Angle BCA = angle DAC — alternate angles, because AD is parallel to BC
  4. Two angles and the side between them are equal, so triangle ABC is congruent to triangle CDA (ASA)
(b)Explain why AB = CD.
  1. In the congruence ABC ↔ CDA, the side AB corresponds to the side CD.
  2. AB = CD because corresponding sides of congruent triangles are equal. — this is why the opposite sides of a parallelogram are equal
