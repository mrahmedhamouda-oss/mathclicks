Question 5  [4 marks]
(a)Find the equation of the line parallel to y = 2x + 7 that passes through the point (3, 1).
  1. Parallel lines have equal gradients, so the gradient is 2.
  2. y = 2x + c, and (3, 1) lies on it: 1 = 2 × 3 + c.
  3. c = 1 − 6 = −5
  4. y = 2x − 5

  3. y = −1⁄2x + c, and (4, 5) lies on it: 5 = −1⁄2 × 4 + c.
  4. c = 5 + 2 = 7
  5. y = −1⁄2x + 7
