Question 4  [4 marks]
(a)Calculate the mean number of people per car.
Give your answer correct to 3 significant figures.
  1. Multiply each number of people by its frequency and add.
  2. 1×8 + 2×11 + 3×6 + 4×3 + 5×2 = 8 + 22 + 18 + 12 + 10 = 70 — Σfx
  3. There are 8 + 11 + 6 + 3 + 2 = 30 cars. — Σf
  4. Mean = 70 ÷ 30 = 2.333…
  5. Mean = 2.33 (3 s.f.)
(b)One of the 30 cars is chosen at random.
Find the probability that it contains at least 4 people.
Give your answer as a fraction in its simplest form.
  1. ‘At least 4’ means 4 people or 5 people.
  2. Number of such cars = 3 + 2 = 5
  3. Probability = 5⁄30 = 1⁄6 — divide numerator and denominator by 5

Question 10  [6 marks]
(a)Calculate an estimate of the mean time.
  1. Use the midpoint of each class.
  2. Midpoints: 15, 25, 40, 65 — e.g. (30 + 50) ÷ 2 = 40
  3. Σfx = 15×8 + 25×17 + 40×15 + 65×10
  4. = 120 + 425 + 600 + 650 = 1795
  5. Mean = 1795 ÷ 50 = 35.9 seconds
(b)Explain why your answer to part (a) is an estimate.
  1. The table only says which class each time is in, not the actual times.
  2. Every time in a class is replaced by the midpoint of that class, so the total 1795 is not the true total.
  3. The individual times are unknown; midpoints are used, so the mean can only be an estimate.
(c)Two of the 50 runners are chosen at random.
Find the probability that they both took more than 50 seconds.
Give your answer correct to 3 significant figures.
  1. 10 of the 50 runners took more than 50 seconds.
  2. P(first took more than 50 s) = 10⁄50
  3. The runner is not replaced, so P(second) = 9⁄49 — one fewer runner, one fewer of them over 50 s
  4. P(both) = 10⁄50 × 9⁄49 = 90⁄2450 = 9⁄245 = 0.036734…
  5. Probability = 0.0367 (3 s.f.) — exact value 9⁄245

  2. Count the large grid squares: the bar for 10 < a ≤ 15 is 1 large square wide (5 years) and 6 large squares tall, so its area is 6 large squares. — one large square = 5 years by 1 unit of height

  1. Ages 35 to 45 make up 10 of the 20 years in the class 25 < a ≤ 45, and the histogram assumes ages are spread evenly within a class.
  2. Estimate = 10⁄20 × 30 = 15 — or 2 large squares × 5 people
