Question 1  [2 marks]
Calculate the average speed of the coach.
Give your answer in km/h.
  1. 2 hours 30 minutes = 2.5 hours. — the time must be in hours to give a speed in km/h
  2. Average speed = distance ÷ time = 150 ÷ 2.5
  3. Average speed = 60 km/h
  4. Check: 60 × 2.5 = 150 km ✓

Question 3  [3 marks]
(a)Change 90 km/h into metres per second.
  1. 90 km = 90 × 1000 = 90 000 metres, and 1 hour = 3600 seconds.
  2. Speed = 90 000 ÷ 3600
  3. Speed = 25 m/s — dividing by 3.6 is the same short cut
  4. Check: 25 × 3.6 = 90 km/h ✓
(b)Work out the distance the motorcycle travels in 8 seconds.
  1. Distance = speed × time = 25 × 8

Question 6  [4 marks]
(a)Calculate the total time for the journey.
Give your answer in hours.
  1. First stage: time = 90 ÷ 60 = 1.5 hours.
  2. Second stage: time = 100 ÷ 80 = 1.25 hours.
  3. Total time = 1.5 + 1.25 = 2.75 hours — 2 hours 45 minutes
(b)Calculate her average speed for the whole journey.
Give your answer correct to 3 significant figures.
  1. Total distance = 90 + 100 = 190 km.
  2. Average speed = total distance ÷ total time = 190 ÷ 2.75
  3. = 69.09… km/h
  4. Average speed = 69.1 km/h (3 s.f.)
  5. Note: the answer is not (60 + 80) ÷ 2 = 70, because more time is spent at the slower speed.

Question 8  [4 marks]

  1. In one second the cheetah travels 28 m, so in one hour it travels 28 × 3600 = 100 800 m.
  2. 100 800 m = 100 800 ÷ 1000 = 100.8 km.
  3. Top speed = 100.8 km/h
(b)Calculate the time the cheetah takes to run 1 kilometre at this speed.

  3. Time = 35.7 s (3 s.f.)

Question 9  [5 marks]
(a)Work out how far the lorry has travelled by 09 00.
  1. The lorry travels for 1 hour before the car starts.
  2. Distance = 60 × 1 = 60 km
(b)Find the time at which the car catches up with the lorry.
  1. At 09 00 the car is 60 km behind the lorry.
  2. Each hour the car gains 90 − 60 = 30 km. — this is the relative speed of the car compared with the lorry
  3. Time to close the gap = 60 ÷ 30 = 2 hours.
  4. The car catches up at 09 00 + 2 hours = 11 00
  5. Check: at 11 00 the lorry has travelled 60 × 3 = 180 km and the car 90 × 2 = 180 km ✓
(c)Work out how far from the depot the car catches up with the lorry.
  1. The car has been travelling for 2 hours at 90 km/h.
  2. Distance = 90 × 2 = 180 km

Question 10  [6 marks]
(a)Show that x2 + 10x − 2000 = 0.
  1. Time for the first journey = 100⁄x hours. — time = distance ÷ speed
  2. Time for the return journey = 100⁄x + 10 hours.
  3. 30 minutes = 1⁄2 hour, so 100⁄x − 100⁄x + 10 = 1⁄2 — the return journey is the quicker one, so it is subtracted
  4. Multiply every term by 2x(x + 10):
  5. 200(x + 10) − 200x = x(x + 10)
  6. 2000 = x2 + 10x
  7. x2 + 10x − 2000 = 0 — as required
(b)Solve x2 + 10x − 2000 = 0 to find the average speed on the first journey.
  1. Factorise: two numbers with product −2000 and sum +10 are +50 and −40.
  2. (x + 50)(x − 40) = 0
  3. x = −50  or  x = 40
  4. A speed cannot be negative, so x = 40 km/h
(c)Work out the time taken for the first journey.
Give your answer in hours.
  1. Time = 100 ÷ 40 = 2.5 hours
  2. Check: the return journey takes 100 ÷ 50 = 2 hours, which is 30 minutes less ✓
