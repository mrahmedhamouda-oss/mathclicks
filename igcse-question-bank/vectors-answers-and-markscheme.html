Question 10  [6 marks]
(a)Find OM in terms of a and b.
  1. OM = a + ½(b − a) — M is the midpoint of AB
  2. OM = ½(a + b)
(b)Show that OG = 1⁄3(a + b).
  1. OG : GM = 2 : 1, so OG is 2⁄3 of OM. — OM is divided into 3 equal parts
  2. OG = 2⁄3 × ½(a + b)
  3. OG = 1⁄3(a + b) — as required
(c)Show that A, G and N lie on a straight line, and write down the ratio AG : GN.
  1. AG = 1⁄3(a + b) − a = −2⁄3a + 1⁄3b

  3. 3⁄2 × AG = −a + ½b = AN
  4. So AN = 3⁄2AG: the two vectors are parallel and share the point A, so A, G and N are collinear. — parallel vectors through a common point lie on one line
  5. AG is 2⁄3 of AN, so AG : GN = 2 : 1
