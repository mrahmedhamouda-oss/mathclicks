Question 18  [3 marks]
(a)Solve sin x = 0.62.
Give your answers correct to 1 decimal place.
  1. x = sin−1(0.62) = 38.316…° — the calculator gives the acute solution
  2. The sine of an obtuse angle equals the sine of its supplement: sin(180° − x) = sin x. — the sine graph is symmetrical about x = 90°
  3. Second solution = 180 − 38.316… = 141.683…°
  4. x = 38.3° or x = 141.7° (1 d.p.)
(b)Solve cos x = −0.35.
Give your answer correct to 1 decimal place.
  1. cos x is negative, so x is obtuse. — cos is negative between 90° and 180°
  2. x = cos−1(−0.35) = 110.487…° — there is only one solution between 0° and 180°
  3. x = 110.5° (1 d.p.)
