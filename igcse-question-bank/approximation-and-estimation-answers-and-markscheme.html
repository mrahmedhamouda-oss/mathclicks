Question 10  [6 marks]
(a)Calculate the upper bound of y.
Give your answer correct to 3 significant figures.
  1. Bounds: 27.35 ≤ a < 27.45, 12.55 ≤ b < 12.65, 4.95 ≤ c < 5.05. — each is correct to 1 d.p., so half the unit is 0.05
  2. y is greatest when the numerator is greatest and the denominator is least.
  3. Upper bound = (27.45 + 12.65)⁄4.95 = 40.1⁄4.95 = 8.10101…
  4. Upper bound of y = 8.10 (3 s.f.)
(b)Calculate the lower bound of y.
Give your answer correct to 3 significant figures.
  1. y is least when the numerator is least and the denominator is greatest.
  2. Lower bound = (27.35 + 12.55)⁄5.05 = 39.9⁄5.05 = 7.90099…
  3. Lower bound of y = 7.90 (3 s.f.)
(c)Write down the value of y to an appropriate degree of accuracy.
Give a reason for your answer.
  1. The two bounds are 7.90099… and 8.10101… .
  2. Correct to 1 significant figure both bounds are 8; correct to 2 significant figures they are 7.9 and 8.1, which do not agree. — compare the bounds figure by figure
  3. y = 8, because the upper and lower bounds agree only to 1 significant figure. — only figures common to both bounds can be relied on
