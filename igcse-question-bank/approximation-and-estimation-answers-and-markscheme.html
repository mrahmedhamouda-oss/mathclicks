  2. Lower bound = (27.35 + 12.55)⁄5.05 = 39.9⁄5.05 = 7.90099…

Question 25  [6 marks]
(a)Show that L = (P − 2W)⁄2, where P is the perimeter and W is the width.
  1. Perimeter of a rectangle: P = 2L + 2W — two lengths and two widths
  2. 2L = P − 2W — subtract 2W from both sides
  3. L = (P − 2W)⁄2 — divide both sides by 2
(b)Calculate the upper bound of L.
  1. P lies between 29.5 and 30.5; W lies between 6.35 and 6.45.
  2. L is greatest when P is greatest and W is least. — subtracting 2W, so the bound for W swaps over
  3. Upper bound = (30.5 − 2 × 6.35)⁄2 = (30.5 − 12.7)⁄2 = 17.8⁄2
  4. Upper bound of L = 8.9 cm
(c)Calculate the lower bound of L.
  1. L is least when P is least and W is greatest.
  2. Lower bound = (29.5 − 2 × 6.45)⁄2 = (29.5 − 12.9)⁄2 = 16.6⁄2
  3. Lower bound of L = 8.3 cm — check: the nominal length (30 − 12.8) ÷ 2 = 8.6 lies between 8.3 and 8.9 ✓
(d)Amal says the length could be exactly 8.9 cm.
Explain why Amal is wrong.
  1. The perimeter satisfies 29.5 ≤ P < 30.5, so P can never actually equal 30.5. — the upper bound is not included in the error interval
  2. Therefore L < 8.9 always: the length cannot be exactly 8.9 cm — 8.9 is the upper bound, which is approached but never reached
