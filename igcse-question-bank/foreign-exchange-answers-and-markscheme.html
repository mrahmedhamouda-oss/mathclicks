Question 1  [2 marks]
(a)Change 250 euros into dollars.
  1. Euros → dollars, so multiply by 1.15. — one euro is worth more than one dollar, so the number of dollars must be larger
  2. 250 × 1.15 = $287.50

  1. Dollars → euros, so divide by 1.15. — this is the reverse of part (a)
  2. 92 ÷ 1.15 = €80
  3. Check: 80 × 1.15 = 92 ✓

Question 3  [3 marks]
(a)Work out the number of pesos she receives.
  1. Dollars → pesos, so multiply by 17.6.
  2. 500 × 17.6 = 8800 pesos
(b)Nadia spends 6200 pesos. At the end of her holiday she changes all the pesos she has left back into dollars, when the exchange rate is 1 dollar = 17.9 pesos.

Give your answer correct to 2 decimal places.
  1. Pesos remaining = 8800 − 6200 = 2600 pesos.

Question 6  [4 marks]
(a)Calculate the percentage decrease in the value of the pound against the dollar from Monday to Friday.
Give your answer correct to 3 significant figures.
  1. Decrease in value = 1.32 − 1.29 = 0.03 dollars per pound.
  2. Percentage decrease = 0.03⁄1.32 × 100 — percentage change is always measured against the original value
  3. = 2.2727…
  4. = 2.27% (3 s.f.)
(b)Sam changes 600 dollars into pounds on Friday instead of on Monday.
Calculate how many more pounds he receives.
Give your answer correct to 2 decimal places.
  1. On Monday: 600 ÷ 1.32 = £454.5454… — dollars → pounds, so divide by the rate
  2. On Friday: 600 ÷ 1.29 = £465.1162…
  3. Difference = 465.1162… − 454.5454… = 10.5708…
  4. He receives £10.57 more (2 d.p.) — the pound is weaker on Friday, so each dollar buys more pounds

Question 7  [4 marks]
(a)Find the exchange rate 1 euro = k yuan.
Give the value of k correct to 2 decimal places.
  1. Take 1 dollar as the link between the two currencies.
  2. 1 dollar = 0.92 euros and 1 dollar = 7.80 yuan, so 0.92 euros = 7.80 yuan. — both are worth exactly one dollar
  3. 1 euro = 7.80 ÷ 0.92 = 8.4782… yuan — divide both sides by 0.92
  4. k = 8.48 (2 d.p.)
(b)Change 2500 yuan into euros.
Give your answer correct to 2 decimal places.
  1. Yuan → dollars: 2500 ÷ 7.80 = 320.512… dollars.
  2. Dollars → euros: 320.512… × 0.92 = 294.871…
  3. = €294.87 (2 d.p.) — use the unrounded rate throughout, then round only at the end

Question 8  [4 marks]
(a)Calculate the number of dollars Yara receives.
  1. Yara is buying dollars, so the bank is selling them: use 1 dollar = 31.2. — always use the rate for the direction the bank is trading
  2. 9360 ÷ 31.2 = $300
(b)Her trip is cancelled, so she immediately sells all of these dollars back to the same bank.
Calculate how many Egyptian pounds she has lost.
  1. Selling the dollars back, the bank buys them at 1 dollar = 30.6.
  2. 300 × 30.6 = 9180 Egyptian pounds.
  3. Loss = 9360 − 9180 = 180 Egyptian pounds — the gap between the buying and selling rates is how the bank makes its money

Calculate the number of shillings she receives.
  1. Euros left = 600 − 215 = 385 euros.
  2. Euros → shillings, so multiply by 142.5.
  3. 385 × 142.5 = 54 862.5 shillings

  3. = £69.11 (2 d.p.)
