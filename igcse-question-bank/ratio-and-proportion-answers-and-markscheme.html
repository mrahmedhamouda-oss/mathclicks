Question 1  [2 marks]
(a)24 : 36
  1. The highest common factor of 24 and 36 is 12.
  2. 24 ÷ 12 = 2 and 36 ÷ 12 = 3 — divide both parts by the HCF
  3. 24 : 36 = 2 : 3
(b)45 : 60 : 75
  1. The highest common factor of 45, 60 and 75 is 15.
  2. 45 ÷ 15 = 3, 60 ÷ 15 = 4 and 75 ÷ 15 = 5
  3. 45 : 60 : 75 = 3 : 4 : 5

Question 3  [3 marks]

  1. The mass is directly proportional to the number of books, so find the mass of one book.
  2. 1 book: 1.35 ÷ 6 = 0.225 kg — the unitary method
  3. 10 books: 10 × 0.225 = 2.25 kg
(b)Find the number of these books that have a total mass of 3.6 kg.

Question 7  [4 marks]

Question 8  [4 marks]
(a)Work out the number of boys in the club.
  1. Let the original numbers be 5k boys and 3k girls. — writing the ratio 5 : 3 with a common multiplier
  2. After the 12 girls join: 5k : (3k + 12) = 5 : 4
  3. 4 × 5k = 5 × (3k + 12) — cross-multiply
  4. 20k = 15k + 60, so 5k = 60 and k = 12
  5. Number of boys = 5k = 60
(b)Work out the total number of members after the 12 girls join.
  1. Girls at the start = 3 × 12 = 36, so after 12 join there are 48 girls.
  2. Total = 60 + 48 = 108 members
  3. Check: 60 : 48 = 5 : 4 ✓ and 60 : 36 = 5 : 3 ✓

Question 9  [5 marks]
(a)Find A : B : C, giving your answer in its simplest form.
  1. B appears in both ratios, as 4 and as 6; the lowest common multiple of 4 and 6 is 12.
  2. A : B = 3 : 4 = 9 : 12 — multiply both parts by 3
  3. B : C = 6 : 5 = 12 : 10 — multiply both parts by 2
  4. A : B : C = 9 : 12 : 10 — the parts have no common factor, so this is simplest form
(b)Work out the number of tonnes farm A receives.
  1. C is 10 parts and C = 250 tonnes.
  2. 1 part = 250 ÷ 10 = 25 tonnes
  3. A = 9 × 25 = 225 tonnes
  4. Check: A : B = 225 : 300 = 3 : 4 ✓
(c)Work out the total number of tonnes in the harvest.
  1. Total = (9 + 12 + 10) parts = 31 × 25
  2. Total = 775 tonnes

Question 10  [6 marks]

  1. Inversely proportional to the square of d means F = k ÷ d2.
  2. 12 = k ÷ 52 = k ÷ 25 — substitute d = 5, F = 12
  3. k = 12 × 25 = 300
  4. F = 300 ÷ d2
(b)Calculate F when d = 8. Give your answer correct to 3 significant figures.
  1. F = 300 ÷ 82 = 300 ÷ 64 = 4.6875
  2. F = 4.69 (3 s.f.)

  1. 48 = 300 ÷ d2
  2. d2 = 300 ÷ 48 = 6.25 — multiply by d2, then divide by 48
  3. d = √6.25 = 2.5
  4. Check: 300 ÷ 2.52 = 300 ÷ 6.25 = 48 ✓
