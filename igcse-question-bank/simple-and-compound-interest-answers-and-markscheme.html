Question 2  [3 marks]
(a)Calculate the value of his investment at the end of the 2 years.
  1. Increasing by 3% means multiplying by 1.03 each year. — 100% + 3% = 103% = 1.03
  2. Value = 2000 × 1.032 — one factor of 1.03 for each year
  3. = 2000 × 1.0609 = $2121.80

(b)Write down the interest he receives.
  1. Interest = final value − amount invested.
  2. 2121.80 − 2000 = $121.80 — this is more than the $120 that simple interest would give

Question 5  [4 marks]
(a)Calculate the value of the car after 3 years.
  1. A decrease of 15% means multiplying by 1 − 0.15 = 0.85 each year. — 100% − 15% = 85%
  2. Value = 18 000 × 0.853 = 18 000 × 0.614125
  3. Value = $11 054.25
(b)Work out the loss in value of the car over the 3 years.
  1. Loss = 18 000 − 11 054.25
  2. Loss = $6945.75
(c)Calculate the overall percentage decrease in the value of the car.
Give your answer correct to 3 significant figures.
  1. Percentage decrease = loss⁄original value × 100
  2. = 6945.75⁄18 000 × 100 = 38.5875
  3. Percentage decrease = 38.6% (3 s.f.) — not 45%: each year’s 15% is taken from a smaller amount

Question 6  [4 marks]

  2. Interest for 4 years = 4 × 150 = $600.

(b)Calculate the value of Tomas’s investment after 4 years.

Question 10  [6 marks]
(a)Calculate the value of r.
Give your answer correct to 1 decimal place.
  1. Let the yearly multiplier be k, so 12 000 × k5 = 15 315.38.
  2. k5 = 15 315.38 ÷ 12 000 = 1.27628…
  3. k = 5√1.27628… = 1.05000… — take the 5th root of the multiplier
  4. The multiplier 1.05 means an increase of 0.05 = 5%.
  5. r = 5.0
  6. Check: 12 000 × 1.055 = $15 315.38 ✓
