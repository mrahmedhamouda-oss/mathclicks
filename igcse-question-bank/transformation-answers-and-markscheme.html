Question 5  [4 marks]

  1. A and B are the same size but B is ‘back to front’, so the transformation is a reflection.
  2. (1, 1) maps to (11, 1), so the mirror line is halfway between them, at x = (1 + 11) ÷ 2 = 6. — the mirror line is the perpendicular bisector of each pair of matching points
  3. (3, 1) maps to (9, 1), and (3 + 9) ÷ 2 = 6 as well ✓

  4. Translation by the vector 4
    −6     — a full description of a translation needs the column vector

Question 25  [5 marks]
(a)Triangle P is rotated through 90° clockwise about the point (1, 1) to give triangle Q.
Find the coordinates of the vertices of triangle Q.
  1. Measure each vertex from the centre (1, 1); a 90° clockwise turn sends ‘a right, b up’ to ‘b right, a down’. — (x, y) → (y, −x) relative to the centre
  2. (2, 2): 1 right, 1 up → 1 right, 1 down → (2, 0)
  3. (5, 2): 4 right, 1 up → 1 right, 4 down → (2, −3)
  4. (2, 3): 1 right, 2 up → 2 right, 1 down → (3, 0)
  5. Vertices of Q: (2, 0), (2, −3) and (3, 0)
(b)Triangle Q is reflected in the line y = 1 to give triangle R.
Write down the coordinates of the vertices of triangle R.
  1. Reflection in y = 1: (x, y) → (x, 2 − y).
  2. Vertices of R: (2, 2), (2, 5) and (3, 2)
(c)Describe fully the single transformation that maps triangle P directly onto triangle R.
  1. Compare P with R: (2, 2) → (2, 2), (5, 2) → (2, 5), (2, 3) → (3, 2). The coordinates have simply been swapped. — a rotation followed by a reflection is always a reflection
  2. Reflection in the line y = x — check: (2, 2) lies on y = x and is invariant ✓
