(a)Triangle T is translated by the vector 5
2.

  3. Image of B = (−4, 1) — it is the same distance from the axis, on the other side

Question 2  [3 marks]
(a)Write down the coordinates of the image of P after reflection in the x‑axis.
  1. Reflection in the x‑axis: (x, y) → (x, −y).
  2. Image = (3, −2) — P is 2 above the axis, so the image is 2 below
(b)Write down the coordinates of the image of P after reflection in the line x = 5.
  1. P is 5 − 3 = 2 units to the left of the line x = 5.

  3. Image = (7, 2)
(c)Write down the coordinates of the image of P after reflection in the line y = x.
  1. Reflection in y = x swaps the two coordinates: (x, y) → (y, x).
  2. Image = (2, 3)

Question 4  [4 marks]
(a)Write down the coordinates of the image of P after a rotation of 90° anticlockwise about the origin.
  1. A rotation of 90° anticlockwise about the origin sends (x, y) to (−y, x).
  2. (2, 5) → (−5, 2)
  3. Image = (−5, 2) — both points are √29 from the origin
(b)Write down the coordinates of the image of P after a rotation of 180° about the origin.
  1. A rotation of 180° about the origin sends (x, y) to (−x, −y). — a half turn
  2. Image = (−2, −5)
(c)Find the coordinates of the image of P after a rotation of 90° clockwise about the point (1, 1).
  1. Work out the position of P relative to the centre (1, 1).
  2. P is 2 − 1 = 1 to the right and 5 − 1 = 4 up from the centre.
  3. A 90° clockwise turn sends ‘1 right and 4 up’ to ‘4 right and 1 down’. — (x, y) → (y, −x) relative to the centre
  4. Image = (1 + 4, 1 − 1) = (5, 0)
  5. Check: both P and its image are √17 from (1, 1) ✓

Question 5  [4 marks]
(a)Describe fully the single transformation that maps triangle A onto triangle B.
  1. A and B are the same size but B is ‘back to front’, so the transformation is a reflection.
  2. (1, 1) maps to (11, 1), so the mirror line is halfway between them, at x = (1 + 11) ÷ 2 = 6. — the mirror line is the perpendicular bisector of each pair of matching points
  3. (3, 1) maps to (9, 1), and (3 + 9) ÷ 2 = 6 as well ✓
  4. Reflection in the line x = 6 — a full description of a reflection needs the equation of the mirror line
(b)Describe fully the single transformation that maps triangle A onto triangle C.
  1. A and C are the same size and the same way round, and C is not turned, so the transformation is a translation.
  2. (1, 1) maps to (5, −5): 4 to the right and 6 down.
  3. The other vertices move in the same way, e.g. (1, 4) → (5, −2) ✓
  4. Translation by the vector 4
    −6     — a full description of a translation needs the column vector

Question 6  [4 marks]
(a)Find the coordinates of the three vertices of the image.
  1. For an enlargement, the distance of each point from the centre is multiplied by the scale factor.
  2. (2, 1) is 1 right and 1 up from (1, 0); doubling gives 2 right and 2 up, so the image is (3, 2).
  3. (4, 1) is 3 right and 1 up; doubling gives 6 right and 2 up, so the image is (7, 2).
  4. (2, 4) is 1 right and 4 up; doubling gives 2 right and 8 up, so the image is (3, 8).
  5. Vertices of the image: (3, 2), (7, 2) and (3, 8)
(b)The area of triangle A is 3 cm2.
Write down the area of the image.
  1. Lengths are multiplied by 2, so areas are multiplied by 22 = 4.
  2. Area of the image = 3 × 4 = 12 cm2

Question 7  [4 marks]
(a)Find the coordinates of the three vertices of triangle B.
  1. A negative scale factor puts the image on the opposite side of the centre and turns it upside down.
  2. Multiply each coordinate by −2, because the centre is the origin.
  3. (1, 1) → (−2, −2)   and   (3, 1) → (−6, −2)
  4. (1, 2) → (−2, −4)
  5. Vertices of B: (−2, −2), (−6, −2) and (−2, −4)

  3. Enlargement, scale factor −1⁄2, centre (0, 0) — check: (−6, −2) × (−1⁄2) = (3, 1) ✓

Question 9  [5 marks]
(a)Describe fully the single transformation that maps triangle A onto triangle B.
  1. A and B are congruent and B is turned, not flipped, so the transformation is a rotation. — the shape is the same way round
  2. The right angle at (1, 1) has become a right angle at (1, 3), and the side of length 2 has turned from horizontal to vertical, so the turn is 90°.
  3. The centre of rotation is the same distance from each point and its image; the perpendicular bisector of (3, 1)→(1, 5) and of (1, 1)→(1, 3) meet at (0, 2). — the centre is the only point that does not move
  4. Turning (1, 1) about (0, 2): it is 1 right and 1 down from the centre, and 90° anticlockwise sends this to 1 right and 1 up, giving (1, 3) ✓
  5. Rotation of 90° anticlockwise about (0, 2) — a full description of a rotation needs the angle, the direction and the centre
(b)Triangle B is reflected in the x‑axis to give triangle C.
Write down the coordinates of the vertices of triangle C.
  1. Reflection in the x‑axis: (x, y) → (x, −y).
  2. (1, 3) → (1, −3),   (1, 5) → (1, −5),   (0, 3) → (0, −3)
  3. Vertices of C: (1, −3), (1, −5) and (0, −3)

Question 10  [6 marks]
(a)Find the coordinates of the vertices of triangle U.
  1. The centre is the origin, so multiply both coordinates of each vertex by −1⁄2.
  2. (2, 2) → (−1, −1)
  3. (6, 2) → (−3, −1)
  4. (2, 4) → (−1, −2)
  5. Vertices of U: (−1, −1), (−3, −1) and (−1, −2) — the negative sign turns the triangle through 180° about the origin
(b)The area of triangle T is 4 square units.
Work out the area of triangle U.
  1. Areas are multiplied by the square of the scale factor.
  2. (−1⁄2)2 = 1⁄4 — the square of a negative number is positive
  3. Area of U = 4 × 1⁄4 = 1 square unit
  4. Check: U has base 2 and height 1, and 1⁄2 × 2 × 1 = 1 ✓
(c)Write down the scale factor of the enlargement, centre (0, 0), that maps triangle U onto triangle T.
  1. The inverse of an enlargement of scale factor k is an enlargement of scale factor 1⁄k with the same centre.
  2. 1 ÷ (−1⁄2) = −2 — check: (−1, −1) × (−2) = (2, 2) ✓
