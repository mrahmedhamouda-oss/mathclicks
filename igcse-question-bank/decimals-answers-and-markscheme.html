Question 1  [2 marks]
(a)Write these numbers in order of size, starting with the smallest.
  1. Give every number the same number of decimal places: 0.505, 0.550, 0.500, 0.055. — comparing digits place by place is only safe when the decimals are the same length
  2. Compare the tenths first, then the hundredths, then the thousandths.
  3. Order: 0.055, 0.5, 0.505, 0.55 — smallest first, as asked
(b)Write down the value of the digit 7 in the number 4.0072.
  1. 4.0072 = 4 + 0⁄10 + 0⁄100 + 7⁄1000 + 2⁄10000 — writing the number in place-value form
  2. The 7 is in the thousandths column, so its value is 0.007

Question 2  [3 marks]
(a)Work out 3.6 × 0.05.
  1. 3.6 × 0.05: first work out 36 × 5 = 180. — remove the decimal points and multiply the whole numbers
  2. 3.6 has 1 decimal place and 0.05 has 2, so the answer has 1 + 2 = 3 decimal places. — the number of decimal places in a product is the total of those in the factors
  3. 3.6 × 0.05 = 0.18 — 180 with 3 decimal places is 0.180
(b)Work out 0.72 ÷ 0.008.
  1. 0.72 ÷ 0.008 = 0.72⁄0.008
  2. Multiply top and bottom by 1000: = 720⁄8 — making the divisor a whole number does not change the value of the fraction
  3. 720 ÷ 8 = 90
  4. Check: 90 × 0.008 = 0.72 ✓

Question 4  [4 marks]
(a)Write 7⁄8 as a decimal.
  1. 7⁄8 means 7 ÷ 8.
  2. Short division: 7.000 ÷ 8 gives 0.875 exactly. — the denominator 8 = 2³ has only 2 as a prime factor, so the decimal terminates
  3. 7⁄8 = 0.875
(b)Write 5⁄11 as a recurring decimal.
  1. 5⁄11 means 5 ÷ 11.
  2. 5.0000 ÷ 11 = 0.454545… — the remainders 5, 6, 5, 6, … repeat. — 11 is not a factor of any power of 10, so the decimal recurs
  3. 5⁄11 = 0.4̇5̇ = 0.454545… — the dots mark the block that repeats
(c)Write 0.375 as a fraction. Give your answer in its simplest form.
  1. 0.375 = 375⁄1000 — three decimal places, so the denominator is 1000
  2. Divide top and bottom by 125: 375⁄1000 = 3⁄8 — 125 is the highest common factor of 375 and 1000
  3. 0.375 = 3⁄8
(d)Write down which is larger, 5⁄11 or 0.45. Give a reason for your answer.
  1. 5⁄11 = 0.454545… and 0.45 = 0.450000…
  2. The tenths and hundredths agree (0.45…), but the thousandths digit is 5 for 5⁄11 and 0 for 0.45.
  3. So 5⁄11 is larger, because 0.4545… > 0.4500

Question 7  [4 marks]
(a)Show that 0.2̇7̇ = 3⁄11.
  1. Let x = 0.272727… — the repeating block has 2 digits
  2. Multiply by 100: 100x = 27.272727… — multiplying by 10² shifts the decimal point past exactly one repeating block
  3. Subtract: 100x − x = 27.272727… − 0.272727… = 27 — the recurring tails cancel exactly
  4. 99x = 27, so x = 27⁄99
  5. Divide top and bottom by 9: x = 3⁄11 as required — 9 is the highest common factor of 27 and 99
(b)Write 0.31̇8̇ as a fraction. Give your answer in its simplest form.
  1. Let x = 0.3181818… — only the block ‘18’ repeats
  2. Multiply by 100: 100x = 31.8181818…
  3. Subtract: 100x − x = 31.8181818… − 0.3181818… = 31.5
  4. 99x = 31.5, so x = 31.5⁄99 = 315⁄990 — multiply top and bottom by 10
  5. Divide top and bottom by 45: x = 7⁄22
  6. Check: 7 ÷ 22 = 0.31818… ✓

Question 9  [5 marks]
(a)Write N as a fraction. Give your answer in its simplest form.
  1. Let N = 0.727272… — the repeating block has 2 digits
  2. 100N = 72.727272…
  3. 100N − N = 72.727272… − 0.727272… = 72
  4. 99N = 72, so N = 72⁄99
  5. Divide top and bottom by 9: N = 8⁄11
(b)Work out N + 1⁄22. Give your answer as a fraction in its simplest form.
  1. 8⁄11 + 1⁄22
  2. The lowest common denominator of 11 and 22 is 22.
  3. = 16⁄22 + 1⁄22 = 17⁄22
  4. 17 and 22 have no common factor, so the answer is 17⁄22 — 17 is prime and is not a factor of 22
(c)Write your answer to part (b) as a decimal, correct to 4 decimal places.
  1. 17 ÷ 22 = 0.7727272…
  2. The fifth decimal place is 2, so the fourth decimal place stays as 7. — round down when the next digit is 0–4
  3. 17⁄22 = 0.7727 (4 d.p.)

Question 10  [6 marks]
(a)Show that x = 5⁄11.
  1. Let x = 0.454545… — the repeating block ‘45’ has 2 digits
  2. Multiply by 10² = 100: 100x = 45.454545…
  3. Subtract: 100x − x = 45.454545… − 0.454545… = 45 — the recurring tails are identical, so they cancel
  4. 99x = 45, so x = 45⁄99
  5. Divide top and bottom by 9: x = 5⁄11 as required
(b)Show that y = 2⁄15.
  1. Let y = 0.13333… — here only the 3 repeats, and it starts one place late
  2. Multiply by 10: 10y = 1.3333… — this lines the recurring parts up
  3. Multiply by 100: 100y = 13.3333…
  4. Subtract: 100y − 10y = 13.3333… − 1.3333… = 12
  5. 90y = 12, so y = 12⁄90
  6. Divide top and bottom by 6: y = 2⁄15 as required
(c)Work out x − y. Give your answer as a fraction in its simplest form.
  1. x − y = 5⁄11 − 2⁄15
  2. The lowest common denominator of 11 and 15 is 11 × 15 = 165. — 11 and 15 have no common factor
  3. = 75⁄165 − 22⁄165
  4. = 53⁄165 — 53 is prime, so this fraction is already in its simplest form
  5. Check: 53 ÷ 165 = 0.321212… and 0.454545… − 0.13333… = 0.321212… ✓
