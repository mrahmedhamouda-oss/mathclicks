Question 4  [4 marks]
(a)Write 7⁄8 as a decimal.
  1. 7⁄8 means 7 ÷ 8.
  2. Short division: 7.000 ÷ 8 gives 0.875 exactly. — the denominator 8 = 2³ has only 2 as a prime factor, so the decimal terminates
  3. 7⁄8 = 0.875
(b)Write 5⁄11 as a recurring decimal.
  1. 5⁄11 means 5 ÷ 11.
  2. 5.0000 ÷ 11 = 0.454545… — the remainders 5, 6, 5, 6, … repeat. — 11 is not a factor of any power of 10, so the decimal recurs
  3. 5⁄11 = 0.4̇5̇ = 0.454545… — the dots mark the block that repeats
(c)Write 0.375 as a fraction. Give your answer in its simplest form.
  1. 0.375 = 375⁄1000 — three decimal places, so the denominator is 1000
  2. Divide top and bottom by 125: 375⁄1000 = 3⁄8 — 125 is the highest common factor of 375 and 1000
  3. 0.375 = 3⁄8
(d)Write down which is larger, 5⁄11 or 0.45. Give a reason for your answer.
  1. 5⁄11 = 0.454545… and 0.45 = 0.450000…
  2. The tenths and hundredths agree (0.45…), but the thousandths digit is 5 for 5⁄11 and 0 for 0.45.
  3. So 5⁄11 is larger, because 0.4545… > 0.4500
