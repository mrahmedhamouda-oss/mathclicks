Question 23  [5 marks]
(a)Show that triangle ABD is similar to triangle BCD.
  1. Angle ADB = angle BDC = 90°. — BD is perpendicular to AC
  2. Let angle BAD = a. In triangle ABD, angle ABD = 90° − a. — angles in a triangle add up to 180°
  3. Angle DBC = 90° − angle ABD = a. — angle ABC = 90°
  4. So angle BAD = angle CBD and angle ADB = angle BDC.
  5. Two pairs of angles are equal, so triangle ABD is similar to triangle BCD.
(b)Calculate BD.
  1. A ↔ B, B ↔ C, D ↔ D, so AD⁄BD = BD⁄CD — corresponding sides of similar triangles are in the same ratio
  2. 16⁄BD = BD⁄9, so BD2 = 16 × 9 = 144
  3. BD = √144 = 12 cm
(c)Calculate AB.
  1. AB2 = 162 + 122 = 256 + 144 = 400 — Pythagoras’ theorem in triangle ABD
  2. AB = 20 cm
  3. Check: BC = √(92 + 122) = 15 and 202 + 152 = 625 = 252 = AC2 ✓
