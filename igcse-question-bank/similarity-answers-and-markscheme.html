Question 4  [4 marks]
(a)Give a reason why triangle ADE is similar to triangle ABC.
  1. Angle A is common to both triangles.
  2. Angle ADE = angle ABC and angle AED = angle ACB. — corresponding angles, because DE is parallel to BC
  3. All three pairs of angles are equal, so the triangles are similar.
(b)Calculate BC.
  1. AB = 6 + 4 = 10 cm, and AD = 6 cm is the corresponding side.
  2. Scale factor = 10 ÷ 6 = 5⁄3
  3. BC = 9 × 5⁄3 = 15 cm
  4. Check: 9⁄15 = 0.6 = 6⁄10 ✓ — corresponding sides are in the same ratio
(c)Calculate EC.
  1. AC = 7.5 × 5⁄3 = 12.5 cm.
  2. EC = 12.5 − 7.5 = 5 cm — EC is the part of AC beyond E, so subtract AE

Question 7  [4 marks]
(a)The volume of the smaller cone is 480 cm3.
Calculate the volume of the larger cone.
  1. Length scale factor = 18 ÷ 12 = 1.5
  2. Volume scale factor = 1.53 = 3.375 — volumes are enlarged by the cube of the length scale factor
  3. Volume = 480 × 3.375 = 1620 cm3
(b)The curved surface area of the larger cone is 594 cm2.

  1. Area scale factor = 1.52 = 2.25

  3. Area = 594 ÷ 2.25 = 264 cm2
  4. Check: 264 × 2.25 = 594 ✓

Question 9  [5 marks]
(a)Calculate QR.
  1. Triangles PXY and PQR are similar. — angle P is common and XY is parallel to QR, so the corresponding angles are equal
  2. PQ = 8 + 6 = 14 cm, so the scale factor = 14 ÷ 8 = 1.75
  3. QR = 10 × 1.75 = 17.5 cm
(b)The area of triangle PXY is 32 cm2.
Calculate the area of triangle PQR.
  1. Area scale factor = 1.752 = 3.0625
  2. Area of triangle PQR = 32 × 3.0625 = 98 cm2
  3. Check: 32⁄98 = 16⁄49 = (4⁄7)2 = (8⁄14)2 ✓
(c)Calculate the area of the trapezium XQRY.
  1. The trapezium is what is left when triangle PXY is removed from triangle PQR.
  2. Area = 98 − 32 = 66 cm2
