Question 8  [4 marks]
(a)Calculate the value of the van at the end of 3 years.
Give your answer correct to 3 significant figures.
  1. Each year the van keeps 100 − 18 = 82% of its value, so the multiplier is 0.82.
  2. Value = 24 000 × 0.823 — three years of depreciation
  3. = 24 000 × 0.551368 = 13 232.832
  4. Value = $13 200 (3 s.f.) — exact value $13 232.83 to the nearest cent
(b)Calculate the overall percentage decrease in the value of the van over these 3 years.
