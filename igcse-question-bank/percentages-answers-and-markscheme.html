Question 1  [2 marks]
(a)Write Amina’s mark as a percentage.
  1. Percentage = 34⁄40 × 100 — write the score as a fraction of the total, then multiply by 100
  2. = 0.85 × 100 = 85%
(b)Bilal scores 65% in the same test.
Work out Bilal’s mark.
  1. Mark = 65% of 40 = 65⁄100 × 40
  2. = 0.65 × 40 = 26 marks
  3. Check: 26⁄40 = 0.65 = 65% ✓

Question 6  [4 marks]
(a)Calculate the value of her investment at the end of 4 years.
Give your answer correct to the nearest cent.
  1. The multiplier for one year is 1 + 3.5⁄100 = 1.035. — each year the money is 103.5% of the amount at the start of that year
  2. Value = 2400 × 1.0354 — four years of growth
  3. = 2400 × 1.147523… = 2754.0552…
  4. Value = $2754.06 — correct to the nearest cent

  1. Interest = final value − amount invested = 2754.0552… − 2400

Question 7  [4 marks]
(a)Calculate the price of the laptop before the sale.
  1. A 12% reduction leaves 100 − 12 = 88% of the original price.
  2. 0.88 × original price = 528 — 0.88 is the multiplier for a 12% decrease
  3. Original price = 528 ÷ 0.88 = $600
  4. Check: 12% of 600 = $72 and 600 − 72 = $528 ✓
(b)After the sale, the shop increases the sale price of $528 by 12%.
Work out this new price.

Question 8  [4 marks]
(a)Calculate the value of the van at the end of 3 years.
Give your answer correct to 3 significant figures.
  1. Each year the van keeps 100 − 18 = 82% of its value, so the multiplier is 0.82.
  2. Value = 24 000 × 0.823 — three years of depreciation
  3. = 24 000 × 0.551368 = 13 232.832
  4. Value = $13 200 (3 s.f.) — exact value $13 232.83 to the nearest cent
(b)Calculate the overall percentage decrease in the value of the van over these 3 years.
  1. Overall multiplier = 0.823 = 0.551368, so the value left is 55.1368% of the original.
  2. Percentage decrease = 100 − 55.1368 = 44.8632 = 44.9% (3 s.f.) — not 3 × 18 = 54%, because each 18% is of a smaller amount

Question 9  [5 marks]

  1. Selling price = 145% of $14 = 1.45 × 14 — a 45% profit means the multiplier is 1.45

  1. Money from the 90 shirts = 90 × 20.30 = $1827

  4. Check: 1680 × 1.3375 = $2247 ✓

Question 10  [6 marks]
(a)Calculate the value of the house at the end of the second year.
  1. Value = 180 000 × 1.06 × 1.04 — multiply by 1.06 then by 1.04
  2. = 190 800 × 1.04
  3. = $198 432
(b)Calculate the overall percentage increase in the value of the house over the two years.
  1. Overall multiplier = 1.06 × 1.04 = 1.1024
  2. Percentage increase = (1.1024 − 1) × 100 = 10.24% — not 6 + 4 = 10%, because the 4% is taken of the larger amount
  3. Check: 18432⁄180000 × 100 = 10.24 ✓
