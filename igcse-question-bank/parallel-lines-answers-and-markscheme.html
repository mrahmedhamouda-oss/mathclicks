Question 1  [2 marks]
(a)Find the value of x.
  1. Angle QPB = 62° is between the transversal and AB, below AB on the right of PQ.
  2. Angle PQC is above CD on the left of PQ, so the two angles form a ‘Z’ shape. — the two angles are on opposite sides of the transversal
  3. x = 62 — alternate angles are equal
(b)Find the value of y.
  1. Angles QPB and PQD are both between the parallel lines and both on the right of PQ. — they form a ‘C’ shape
  2. 62 + y = 180 — co-interior (allied) angles add up to 180°
  3. y = 180 − 62 = 118
  4. Check: x + y = 62 + 118 = 180 ✓, as CQD is a straight line.

Question 7  [4 marks]
(a)Find angle BCD.
Give a reason for your answer.
  1. AB is parallel to DC and BC is a transversal, so angles ABC and BCD are co-interior.
  2. 68 + angle BCD = 180 — co-interior (allied) angles add up to 180°
  3. Angle BCD = 112°
(b)Write down angle CDA.
  1. AD is parallel to BC and DC is a transversal, so angle CDA and angle BCD are co-interior: angle CDA = 180 − 112.
  2. Angle CDA = 68° — opposite angles of a parallelogram are equal
(c)Find angle DAB.
  1. The four angles of the quadrilateral add up to 360°.
  2. Angle DAB = 360 − 68 − 112 − 68 = 112° — angle sum of a quadrilateral
  3. Check: angle DAB + angle ABC = 112 + 68 = 180 ✓ — co-interior angles for AD parallel to BC
