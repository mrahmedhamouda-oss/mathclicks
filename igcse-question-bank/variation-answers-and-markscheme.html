Question 7  [4 marks]
(a)x is increased by 20%.
Calculate the percentage increase in y.
  1. y = kx2. Increasing x by 20% multiplies x by 1.2. — a 20% increase is a multiplier of 1.2
  2. New y = k(1.2x)2 = 1.44kx2 — square the whole bracket, including the 1.2
  3. So y is multiplied by 1.44.
  4. Percentage increase = (1.44 − 1) × 100 = 44%
(b)t is increased by 25%.
Calculate the percentage decrease in w.
  1. w = k⁄t. Increasing t by 25% multiplies t by 1.25.
  2. New w = k⁄1.25t = 0.8 × k⁄t — 1 ÷ 1.25 = 0.8
  3. So w is multiplied by 0.8.
  4. Percentage decrease = (1 − 0.8) × 100 = 20%

Question 8  [5 marks]
(a)Find the value of F when m = 5 and d = 4.
  1. F = km⁄d2 — combine the two statements into one equation
  2. 12 = k × 3⁄22 = 3k⁄4 — substitute m = 3, d = 2, F = 12
  3. 3k = 48, so k = 16
  4. F = 16 × 5⁄42 = 80⁄16 = 5 — substitute m = 5 and d = 4
(b)Find the positive value of d when F = 20 and m = 10.
Give your answer correct to 3 significant figures.
  1. 20 = 16 × 10⁄d2 = 160⁄d2
  2. d2 = 160 ÷ 20 = 8
  3. d = √8 = 2√2 = 2.8284… — the positive root
  4. d = 2.83 (3 s.f.)

Question 10  [6 marks]
(a)Find a formula for y in terms of x.
  1. y = k(x − 1)3 — the whole bracket is cubed, not just x
  2. 24 = k(3 − 1)3 = k × 23 = 8k — substitute x = 3, y = 24
  3. k = 24 ÷ 8 = 3
  4. y = 3(x − 1)3
(b)Find the value of y when x = 5.
  1. y = 3 × (5 − 1)3 = 3 × 43 = 3 × 64
  2. y = 192

  5. Check: 3 × (1.5 − 1)3 = 3 × 0.125 = 0.375 ✓
