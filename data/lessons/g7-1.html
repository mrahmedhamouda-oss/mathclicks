Core Concepts

1. Diagonals: the tool that turns a polygon into triangles

A polygon is a closed figure made of straight segments. You already know the angles of a triangle add to 180°. Every other polygon can be cut into triangles, and that single idea gives you every formula in this lesson.

Diagonal — a segment that connects any two nonconsecutive vertices of a polygon.
Consecutive vertices — two vertices joined by a side. You cannot draw a diagonal between them; that segment is already a side.
Convex polygon — no interior angle is greater than 180°; every diagonal stays inside the figure.
Regular polygon — all sides congruent and all angles congruent.

In polygon PQRST, the vertices not consecutive with P are R and S. Drawing PR and PS separates the pentagon into three triangles — and the five interior angles of the pentagon are exactly the nine angles of those three triangles, regrouped.

Study Tip — Naming polygons. A polygon with n sides is an n-gon, but several have special names: 3 triangle, 4 quadrilateral, 5 pentagon, 6 hexagon, 7 heptagon, 8 octagon, 9 nonagon, 10 decagon, 11 hendecagon, 12 dodecagon.

2. The interior angle sum

From one vertex of a convex n-gon you can reach every vertex except itself and its two neighbours, so you draw n − 3 diagonals. Those diagonals slice the polygon into n − 2 triangles. Each triangle contributes 180°.

Theorem 7.1 · Polygon Interior Angles Sum Theorem
The sum of the interior angle measures of an n-sided convex polygon is (n − 2) · 180°.
S = (n − 2) · 180°
n = 4 → 2 · 180° = 360°   n = 5 → 3 · 180° = 540°
n = 6 → 4 · 180° = 720°   n = 8 → 6 · 180° = 1080°

The formula runs both ways. Given n, multiply. Given the sum S, solve (n − 2) · 180 = S for n.

3. One interior angle of a regular polygon

If the polygon is regular, all n interior angles are congruent, so share the total equally.

each interior angle = (n − 2) · 180° / n

Reverse it to identify a polygon from one angle. If each interior angle is a°, then the total is a · n, so solve a · n = (n − 2) · 180.

Write a · n = (n − 2) · 180.
Distribute: an = 180n − 360.
Collect: 360 = 180n − an = n(180 − a).
Solve: n = 360 / (180 − a). Notice 180 − a is the exterior angle.

4. The exterior angle sum — always 360°

Extend one side at each vertex. The angle between that extension and the next side is an exterior angle. Walk once around the polygon and you turn through every exterior angle; when you get back to the start you are facing the way you began, so you have turned one full circle.

Theorem 7.2 · Polygon Exterior Angles Sum Theorem
The sum of the exterior angle measures of a convex polygon, one angle at each vertex, is 360°.
m∠1 + m∠2 + m∠3 + m∠4 + m∠5 + m∠6 = 360°

This total does not depend on n. A triangle, a decagon and a 65-gon all have exterior angle sum 360°.

each exterior angle of a regular n-gon = 360° / n
interior angle + exterior angle = 180°  (linear pair)
1
(n − 2) · 180° — interior angle sum of any convex n-gon.
2
360° — exterior angle sum of every convex polygon.
3
360° / n — each exterior angle of a regular n-gon; (n − 2)180° / n — each interior angle.
4
An interior angle and its exterior angle at the same vertex form a linear pair: they add to 180°.
Watch Out! The number of triangles is n − 2, not n. The number of diagonals from one vertex is n − 3. And "360°" is the exterior sum — only divide by n when the polygon is regular.

The measure of an interior angle of a regular polygon is 144°. Find the number of sides in the polygon.

Let n = the number of sides. Because all angles of a regular polygon are congruent, the sum of the interior angle measures is 144n°. By the Polygon Interior Angles Sum Theorem that same sum is (n − 2) · 180°. Set the two expressions equal.

1144n = (n − 2) · 180Write an equation
2144n = 180n − 360Distribute
3−36n = −360Subtract 180n from each side
4n = 10Solve
n = 10 — the polygon is a regular decagon
Check: (10 − 2) · 180° = 1440°, and 1440° ÷ 10 = 144° ✓
Faster route. The exterior angle is 180° − 144° = 36°, and each exterior angle of a regular n-gon is 360°/n. So n = 360 ÷ 36 = 10 in one step.

Find the measure of each exterior angle of a regular dodecagon.

A regular dodecagon has 12 congruent sides and 12 congruent interior angles. The exterior angles are also congruent, because angles supplementary to congruent angles are congruent. Let n = the measure of each exterior angle.

112n = 360Polygon Exterior Angles Sum Thm.
2n = 30Divide each side by 12
Each exterior angle of a regular dodecagon measures 30°
Check: the interior angle is 180° − 30° = 150°, and (12 − 2) · 180° ÷ 12 = 1800° ÷ 12 = 150° ✓

Common mistakes to avoid
  • Using n · 180° instead of (n − 2) · 180°.
  • Dividing the interior sum by n when the polygon is not regular — that only gives an average, not an angle.
  • Stopping at x when the question asks for an angle measure.
  • Adding exterior angles to (n − 2)180°; the exterior sum is always 360°.
  • Counting more than one exterior angle at the same vertex — the theorem uses exactly one per vertex.
  • Forgetting that 360/(180 − a) must come out to a whole number ≥ 3; if it does not, no such regular polygon exists.
